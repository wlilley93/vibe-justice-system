## Recitals

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 18 of the Ministries and Offices Act 2026 (Bill 5) (as inserted by section 14(2) of the Statutory Instruments (Framework) Act 2026 (Bill 26)), the parent authority being the Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills (MBES), which hosts Repositories House for the operational detail of the registrar-of-repositories engineering it owns under the VJS (Constitution and Machinery) Act 2026 (Bill 27); read with the delegated authority of the Delegated Legislative Authority, Law Reform and Codification Act 2026 (Bill 14); in alignment with the security laws of the realm, namely the Security and Integrity Act 2026 (Bill 21) and the durable principles of the Agent Security Instrument 2026 ([2026] REALM-SI 5), for the skill-signing gate and the egress gate operated by the Ministry of Data Security (MDS); conforming to the binding holdings of the Privy Council on the formation of County Courts ([2026] REALM-PC 14), on the agent-security forks ([2026] REALM-PC 13), on the derived-projection rule ([2026] REALM-PC 4), and on one-home-no-fracture ([2026] REALM-PC 8); and as the Standing Committee makes statutory instruments in exercise of the enabling power conferred on the parent authority by section 5C of the VJS (Constitution and Machinery) Act 2026 (Bill 27), the Standing Committee of the Legislature makes the following Regulations.

## PART 1 - INTERPRETATION AND SCOPE

### 1. Definitions

In these Regulations:

**"Repositories House"** (or "Repos House") means the realm's registrar of repositories, a function of the Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills (MBES) and not a governance organ, court, ministry, apex, or citation series of its own; it comprises the set of derived registers described in these Regulations and is built inside the MBES tree as a function of that Ministry, never as a top-level organ (Bill 5 s.3; Bill 20 s.10(3); [2026] REALM-PC 8).

**"a register"** means any one of the derived projections maintained by Repositories House under these Regulations, namely the incorporation register (section 5), the project register (section 7), the skills registry (section 8), and the egress register (section 11).

**"a derived projection"** means a wholly derived, pointer-only record that is deterministically rebuildable from the committed markdown and regenerated in lockstep with its source, and that is never the store of any ratio, status, or citation as authority ([2026] REALM-PC 4 cond.1, as applied by [2026] REALM-PC 14; Bill 20 s.9).

**"the committed tree"** means the staged or committed content of a repository over which a deterministic, zero-token function operates, being the authoritative subject-matter from which every register is projected.

**"the gate"** means the existing deterministic, fail-closed pre-commit gate of the realm, including the token-free secret-and-fact substance scan of Bill 27 s.7(4) read with the integrity gate of Bill 20 s.19(5); the gate is reused, and no new always-on scanner is created (Bill 21 s.13; [2026] REALM-PC 8; [2026] REALM-PC 14 Disposal).

**"gate-plus-git"** means the composition of the gate and the committed-tree history, the deterministic, zero-token apparatus by which the formation of a County Court and the trust status of a repository are established as facts conferred by no office ([2026] REALM-PC 14 R1, R7).

**"the Ministry of Data Security" (MDS)** means the Ministry of Data Security designated under section 18 of the Ministries and Offices Act 2026 (Bill 5), being the Ministry of Security and Integrity constituted under Bill 21 s.4 read with the nomenclature reconciliation inserted into the Security and Integrity Act 2026 (Bill 21) by section 7 of the VJS (Constitution and Machinery) Act 2026 (Bill 27).

**"the MDS private operational registry"** means the Ministry of Data Security's confidential operational record within the meaning of [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.2(2), holding the signer roster, the key custody, the rotation and revocation procedures, the egress allowlist entries, and the resolver configuration off the face of any public record (Bill 27 s.5A(3); [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.18(2)(a), (d)).

**"the Public Mechanics Charter"** means the public Repositories House Public Mechanics Charter (section 4), which states, for each register, the PRINCIPLE and the SCHEMA only, and never any operational fact.

**"the audited break-glass"** has the meaning given by [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.1: a recorded, reason-bearing, audited, non-standing operator bypass of a fail-closed control that logs who, what, when, and why, defaults off, binds even senior actors, and converts a blocked matter into the forward duty to remediate (Bill 13 s.1, s.7(2), s.14(3)).

**"CONFORMANT-PROJECTION" and "DERIVED-INDEX"** mean the store-neutral status a register bears under Bill 20 s.6, signifying a derived projection that is never the authoritative source.

**"AUTHORITATIVE-SOURCE"** means the status reserved to the committed markdown, the conferral of which on any register is reserved to the Supreme Court (Bill 20 s.14, adopting Bill 16 s.23) and is not reopened by these Regulations.

### 2. Scope and the no-conferral spine

(1) These Regulations specify the principles by which Repositories House registers the deterministic facts of the realm's repositories. They hold PRINCIPLES only and no operative facts: they name no script, path, file format detail, command string, host, signer, key, or allowlist entry, consistent with the public-mechanics rule (Bill 27 s.5A) and the principles-at-law rule (Bill 27).

(2) **The no-conferral spine.** Repositories House is DECLARATORY and RECORDING, never CONSTITUTIVE or CONFERRING. It RECORDS facts that the deterministic chain has already established and DECIDES nothing (Bill 20 s.7, s.8, s.10; [2026] REALM-PC 14). It mints no authority; it indexes provenance (Bill 20 s.10(3)). There is no "create", "grant", "approve", "upgrade", "downgrade", "withhold", or "revoke" verb in any register, and no field whose value an operator sets to make a repository a court or to make a skill or an egress capability authoritative. Any state change in a register SHALL be a faithful read-out of a change in the deterministic facts, never an opinion Repositories House forms (Bill 20 s.10(1) to (2)).

(3) **The derived-projection rule.** Every register is a wholly derived, pointer-only projection within the meaning of section 1, deterministically rebuildable from the committed markdown and regenerated in lockstep with its source ([2026] REALM-PC 4 cond.1; Bill 20 s.9). No register is itself the store of any ratio, status, or citation as authority. Each register bears only CONFORMANT-PROJECTION or DERIVED-INDEX status (Bill 20 s.6) and SHALL NOT bear AUTHORITATIVE-SOURCE, which is reserved to the committed markdown; the relocation question is reserved to the Supreme Court (Bill 20 s.14) and is not reopened here. Trust status in a register is a READ of the gate, never a WRITE by the registrar.

(4) **The public-mechanics / private-facts split.** For each register, the PRINCIPLE and the SCHEMA are PUBLIC and reproducible (section 4); the operational FACTS are PRIVATE and held in a gitignored private store, the security-owned facts living in the MDS private operational registry (section 4(3); Bill 27 s.5A(3)). The public realm holds system data only (Bill 27 s.7; [2026] REALM-PC 14).

(5) **Read together without overlap with [2026] REALM-SI 5.** These Regulations are read together with the Agent Security Instrument 2026 ([2026] REALM-SI 5) WITHOUT OVERLAP, on the read-together-without-overlap rule. The skill-signing inclusion gate is OWNED by [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14 and the egress gate is OWNED by [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.15; these Regulations INDEX those gates and the MDS facts behind them, and SHALL NOT duplicate, displace, or override them. Where these Regulations rely on a gate, [2026] REALM-SI 5 supplies the substantive control and these Regulations record its outcome as a provenance pointer.

(6) **Read together with the formation rule of [2026] REALM-PC 14.** The formation of a County Court is a deterministic, zero-token fact established by gate-plus-git over the committed tree, conferred by no office ([2026] REALM-PC 14 R1, R7). These Regulations record that fact and do not constitute it. The incorporation register is read together with that rule WITHOUT OVERLAP: the rule establishes the fact; the register records it.

(7) These Regulations do not amend, suspend, or relax any Act of the Realm, any CASE-LAW article, the Vibe Procedure Rules, or the bench constitution; any provision so read is void to that extent (Bill 14 s.10, s.12, s.17). They neither raise nor lower the duty of care (CASE-LAW s.5).

(8) **Enforcement is confined to three mechanisms only:** (i) the deterministic fail-closed gate, each gate carrying the audited break-glass so that no control may ever brick the realm (Bill 13 s.5A, s.6(a), s.7(2)); (ii) the soft watchdog reminder for soft operational rules (Bill 13 s.6(b)); and (iii) referral to the single judiciary (Bill 13 s.6(c)). No punitive consequence is available, and no consequence on the merits issues from any of them (Bill 21 s.3, s.5, s.6; Bill 27 s.5B(5); CASE-LAW s.6). Repositories House houses no merits-adjudication and no sanction surface.

## PART 2 - THE INCORPORATION REGISTER

### 3. Incorporation as the recording of a deterministic fact

(1) **Principle: registration of a fact, not the grant of a status.** Incorporation is the registration of a deterministic fact, not the grant of a status. A County Court exists from the moment the three conditions of [2026] REALM-PC 14 Ratio 1 hold over its committed tree, namely:
  - (a) **substance** - a system-data-only tracked record bearing no personal or operational facts (Bill 27 s.7; Bill 22);
  - (b) **seat** - a genuine jurisdiction-repository seated as a local seat of the one judiciary on the one CASE-LAW and the Vibe Procedure Rules and the one bench, taking the handle CC-<repo> on the single citator (CASE-LAW s.22(1), (4)); and
  - (c) **conformance** - the Bill 20 integrity chain holds, the s.19(5) gate holds, and trust status is the zero-token output of gate-plus-git and is not QUARANTINED.

(2) **Principle: the three conditions are the certificate.** Those three conditions ARE the realm's certificate of incorporation. Repositories House issues no certificate that does legal work; at most it renders a derived attestation of a fact that gate-plus-git has already established, declaring and declaring only (Bill 20 s.8; [2026] REALM-PC 14 R1, R7, Reasons IV). The certificate-of-incorporation analogue is the substantive boundary and the section 22 seat recorded on the deterministic chain, not anything Repositories House emits.

(3) **Principle: a mints-no-authority register.** The incorporation register is a derived, pointer-only projection (section 2(3)). A registration row is a pointer entry derived from gate-plus-git at zero model tokens, carrying the repository handle CC-<repo>, the commit and seat evidence, and the deterministic trust status read out of gate-plus-git. The "mints no authority" banner is a literal design constraint: no conferral verb and no operator-set field appears in the register (section 2(2); Bill 20 s.10(1) to (2)).

(4) **Principle: incorporation is recorded here but decided nowhere.** Incorporation surfaces in Repositories House but is decided nowhere, being found by gate-plus-git. There is one home per concept: no second citation series and no parallel place where formation is decided ([2026] REALM-PC 8; [2026] REALM-SC 5).

### 4. The substance check polices content, not the .gitignore

(1) **Principle: the check is over content.** The substance limb of section 3(1)(a) polices the CONTENT of the committed tree, not the presence or absence of any single root token. Incorporation SHALL NOT be keyed on the presence of a .gitignore file, a .justice/ directory, or any single root token taken in isolation from substance and seat; to do so is the form-over-substance approach that [2026] REALM-PC 14 R2 to R4 read with CASE-LAW s.22(4) voids, and which Sumberly J's obiter extends to any single root token taken in isolation.

(2) **Principle: the .gitignore is a presumptive mechanism, not a trigger.** The .gitignore is the presumptive mechanism by which private data is kept off the public tree; its absence where private data is held is a red flag the conformance audit raises, never the trigger of registration.

(3) **Principle: the existing gate performs the check.** The incorporation substance check is a zero-token deterministic function over the committed tree, performed by reusing the existing gate (Bill 27 s.7(4); Bill 20 s.19(5)), and not by a second scanner (Bill 21 s.13; [2026] REALM-PC 8; [2026] REALM-PC 14 Disposal). The gate checks the substance; Repositories House reads the result.

### 5. The register and status read-outs

(1) **Principle: a derived, pointer-only register.** The incorporation register is a derived projection over the realm's repositories (section 2(3)). Its SCHEMA and governance rule are public (the Public Mechanics Charter); the per-repository operational facts are private (section 9).

(2) **Principle: status changes are read-outs.** A status change in the register is a faithful read-out of a change in the deterministic facts: the tree changed, or the gate result flipped. The deterministic gate output flips the displayed status automatically; a repository falling to QUARANTINED needs no decider for the fact.

(3) **Principle: a named-decider spine for human or office-initiated changes.** Any human or office-initiated trust-class change (de-registration or de-certification) requires a NAMED DECIDER, a recorded reasoned act, and a judicial-review route, and is RESTORATIVE-ONLY, never punitive (Bill 20 s.11; CASE-LAW s.6). De-certification of constitutional memory leaves a TOMBSTONE and may not touch a record relied on in a live matter.

(4) **Principle: trust status is read, never written.** Trust status in the register is a READ of the gate and is never a WRITE by the registrar (section 2(3)).

### 6. The Public Mechanics Charter

(1) **Principle: publish principle and schema.** Repositories House SHALL publish a public Repositories House Public Mechanics Charter giving, for each register, the PRINCIPLE (that the realm registers its repositories, projects, and skills and indexes which egress policy a repository runs under) and the SCHEMA (each field's meaning, the update governance rule, and the request and appeal route). Principle and schema are PUBLIC and reproducible (Bill 27 s.5A).

(2) **Principle: operational facts are private.** Operational facts are kept PRIVATE in a gitignored private store, namely per-project hosts, configuration, and spend; per-agent skill provenance; the signer roster and key custody; and egress endpoints and allowlist entries. The store mirrors the existing MDS estate-registry-schema pattern (public schema document plus private contents), the public realm holding system data only (Bill 27 s.7; [2026] REALM-PC 14).

(3) **Principle: security facts live in the MDS registry.** The skills signer roster, the key custody, the rotation and revocation procedures, and the egress allowlist entries with the resolver configuration live in the MDS private operational registry, off the face of any public record ([2026] REALM-SI 5 s.2(2), s.18(2)(a), (d); Bill 27 s.5A(3)).

## PART 3 - THE PROJECT REGISTER

### 7. The project register

(1) **Principle: a derived, pointer-only index.** The project register is a derived, deterministically-rebuildable, pointer-only index over the realm's repositories ([2026] REALM-PC 4). It is MBES engineering that MBES owns (Bill 27 s.5B); its SCHEMA and governance rule are public, and the per-project operational facts (hosts, configuration, spend) are private (section 6).

(2) **Principle: machine-checkable rules may not prescribe remedies.** Machine-checkable rules may govern the deterministic gate but SHALL NEVER prescribe court remedies or sanctions. Enforcement is restorative-only (CASE-LAW s.6; Bill 27 s.5B(5)).

## PART 4 - THE SKILLS REGISTRY

### 8. The self-duty to submit and the intake surface

(1) **Principle: the self-duty to submit is a duty of care, not a Repositories House power.** Every agent bears a continuing duty of reasonable skill and care to submit a new skill for inclusion (CASE-LAW s.4 to s.8), cognate to the realm's own self-submit duties. The duty lives on the agent. Repositories House provides only the INTAKE SURFACE; it confers no power over the duty.

(2) **Principle: non-submission is remedied, never punished.** Non-submission is a falling-below remedied restoratively by submitting the skill, never punished (CASE-LAW s.6).

### 9. The inclusion audit is the [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14 fail-closed signing gate

(1) **Principle: the inclusion audit is the already-enacted load gate.** The inclusion audit is the fail-closed LOAD GATE of [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14, not a fresh Repositories House design. A skill SHALL be cryptographically signed before an agent loads it; the agent RUNTIME verifies the signature FAIL-CLOSED, a missing or invalid signature blocking the load and recording an audit alert ([2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14(1)). The "MDS audit-on-inclusion" is the MDS audited-delegation signing of [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14(2): MDS-audited-and-signed is the precondition of loadability. A skill cannot load until it is MDS-audited and signed.

(2) **Principle: registrable-as-submitted but unloadable.** An unsigned or unaudited skill is REGISTRABLE-AS-SUBMITTED but UNLOADABLE by the fail-closed runtime gate. The pipeline is: submission at the Repositories House intake; then MDS audit (the inclusion review); then signature under the MDS-held revocable trust-root audited delegation; and only then loadable.

(3) **Principle: the gate is at the runtime, not at the registrar.** The load decision is the deterministic verify-signature-against-the-MDS-trust-root step at the runtime; Repositories House is the intake and the provenance index only, recording this skill, this signer or delegation, this revocation state, and this inclusion-audit event as provenance pointers. The two are kept separate; Repositories House does not decide loadability.

(4) **Principle: the inclusion gate carries the audited break-glass.** The inclusion gate carries the audited break-glass ([2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14(3)): a needed-but-unsigned skill is met by a recorded, reason-bearing, non-standing operator bypass (who, what, when, why) that converts to the forward duty to sign and remediate, never a hard brick. Refusal to include is a capability gate, not a sanction (Bill 21 s.6).

(5) **Principle: Repositories House holds no key and is not the trust root.** Signing authority stays with MDS under audited delegation; per-team signers operate within the central framework; the sole single-key model is rejected ([2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14(2); [2026] REALM-PC 13 Fork 3). Repositories House holds NO key, is NOT the trust root, and SHALL NOT become a second place that confers, grades, or withholds skill trust (Bill 20 s.10; [2026] REALM-PC 14).

## PART 5 - EGRESS LICENSING

### 10. Egress licensing is indexing, not conferral

(1) **Principle: licensing is not a Repositories House conferral.** The "licensing" of egress is NOT a conferral of authority by Repositories House; possession of an egress capability is not authority (Bill 20 s.10; Bill 18 s.3). It is the [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.15 regime, operated by MDS and INDEXED by Repositories House.

(2) **Principle: the indexed regime.** The regime operated by MDS under [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.15 is:
  - (a) a deterministic FAIL-CLOSED gate to the policy-defined ALLOWLIST, denying an un-allowlisted destination by default;
  - (b) a genuine evidenced need met by the AUDITED, NON-STANDING break-glass, which logs who, what, when, and why and converts the matter to the forward duty to remediate;
  - (c) a COMPLEMENTARY soft fail-open watchdog for the high-volume reversible egress class (Bill 21 s.13), the second lawful mechanism for that class and never a substitute for the fail-closed gate on the exfiltration class; and
  - (d) all egress logged to the non-derogable record.

(3) **Principle: a break-glass-less hard block is void.** A break-glass-less hard block is VOID ([2026] REALM-SI 5 s.15(2)): the egress surface SHALL expose the audited break-glass for every fail-closed deny, and SHALL NEVER collapse into monitor-only or fail-open on the exfiltration class, sending being an irreversible outward act on the Bill 18 s.2(e) closed list whose statutory default is fail-closed. The rule is block-and-log, never log-instead-of-block, and never block-with-no-bypass.

### 11. The egress register

(1) **Principle: record which policy a repository runs under.** Repositories House RECORDS, per repository, WHICH MDS allowlist policy that repository runs under, and indexes that provenance. It does not itself decide or grant egress (section 2(2); Bill 20 s.10).

(2) **Principle: the facts live in the MDS registry.** The allowlist entries and the resolver configuration live in the MDS private operational registry, off the face of any public record ([2026] REALM-SI 5 s.2(2), s.18(2)(d)). The per-repository, per-agent, and per-tenant egress-policy granularity, and how a County Court's egress policy binds to its [2026] REALM-PC 14 formation record, are reserved to the MDS private operational registry ([2026] REALM-SI 5 s.2(2), s.18(4)) as an operational call.

## PART 6 - MINISTRY OF DATA SECURITY: OPERATE, AUDIT, REFER

### 12. MDS = operate-audit-refer only

(1) **Principle: operate and audit, never adjudicate.** The Ministry of Data Security performs the inclusion audit and operates the skill-signing and egress gates, but MAY NOT find a breach as a binding fact, score, gate on the merits, certify, or sanction; the classifier may not be the container (Bill 21 s.5, s.6; [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.18(3)).

(2) **Principle: refer, never sanction.** A suspected breach (a tampered skill, an exfiltration attempt) is REFERRED to the single judiciary, MDS acting as a party and never as the bench. Containment is pause-and-contain only, reversible, and auto-lifting on cessation of live forward risk (Bill 21 s.8). Repositories House surfaces the MDS audit outcomes, the gate outcomes, and the referral path, and houses no merits-adjudication or sanction surface (section 2(8)).

## PART 7 - IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY AND OPERATIONAL GOVERNANCE

### 13. Mandate to the Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills

(1) The Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills (MBES), the parent authority of these Regulations and the host of Repositories House, is MANDATED to implement, maintain, and remedy the Repositories House registers as the engineering it owns (Bill 27 s.5B).

(2) The mandate extends to:
  - (a) the incorporation register, recording the [2026] REALM-PC 14 R1 three-condition formation fact as a pointer entry read out of gate-plus-git, with the named-decider spine for human or office-initiated changes (sections 3 to 5);
  - (b) the project register as a derived, pointer-only index, schema public and facts private (section 7);
  - (c) the skills registry intake surface and provenance index, indexing the [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.14 signing gate without holding any key or becoming the trust root (sections 8 to 9);
  - (d) the egress register, recording which MDS allowlist policy a repository runs under and indexing the [2026] REALM-SI 5 s.15 regime (sections 10 to 11);
  - (e) the Public Mechanics Charter (the public principle and schema for each register) and the gitignored private store for operational facts, mirroring the MDS estate-registry-schema pattern (section 6); and
  - (f) reusing the existing gate for the incorporation substance scan, and building no second scanner (section 4(3); Bill 21 s.13; [2026] REALM-PC 8).

(3) The mandate is engineering, not law-making and not adjudicatory. It confers no power to author, amend, or enact the authoritative text of these Regulations, to confer any status, to find a breach as a binding fact, to classify on the merits, to score, to gate on the merits, or to sanction; MBES implements and remedies, and adjudication is reserved to the single judiciary (Bill 27 s.5B(5); [2026] REALM-SC 8). The force of these Regulations comes from the Committee's making and not from the hand that drafts or implements them; MBES's policy arm may propose, but the Standing Committee is the sole drafter and the Sovereign the sole enactor (Bill 29; [2026] REALM-SC 8).

(4) A refinement of the operational facts in the private store or the MDS private operational registry is made in the ordinary course; a refinement that alters a durable principle in Parts 1 to 6 is made only by amendment of this Instrument under Part 8.

### 14. Audit and referral

(1) Every registration read-out, every named-decider trust-class change, every inclusion-audit event surfaced as provenance, every gate outcome indexed, and every break-glass use is recorded to the non-derogable record (Bill 8; Bill 21 s.11), so the conduct of the registrar is reviewable on demand.

(2) MBES SHALL report a persistent or unremedied defect in a register to the Ministry of Justice, and any conformance question MAY be referred to the single judiciary on the progression ladder (CASE-LAW s.13), never as a punitive matter but as a governance conformance check (Bill 27 s.5A(4)).

## PART 8 - AMENDMENT AND COMMENCEMENT

### 15. Amendment

(1) Amendment to this Instrument is made by the Standing Committee as a statutory instrument under section 18 of the Ministries and Offices Act 2026 (Bill 5), the parent authority being the Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills (MBES), read with the delegated authority of the Delegated Legislative Authority, Law Reform and Codification Act 2026 (Bill 14), using the Bill 14 s.27 amendment procedure (as substituted by Bill 26 s.6), which re-runs the Bill 14 s.14 objection window, as the registers evolve.

(2) Amendments are published, with the original text and the amending text clearly marked, on the append-with-supersede rule (CASE-LAW Amendment Procedure); silent repeal is never permitted. Each amendment undergoes the Bill 14 s.14 objection window.

(3) This Instrument is subordinate to the Acts of the Realm and to case law and is void to the extent of any conflict with an Act of the Realm or any entrenched article (Bill 14 s.17; CASE-LAW s.1, s.11(f)). Whether any register may bear AUTHORITATIVE-SOURCE is reserved to the Supreme Court (Bill 20 s.14) and is not determined here; until determined, every register is store-neutral CONFORMANT-PROJECTION or DERIVED-INDEX only.

### 16. Commencement

(1) This Instrument comes into force on expiry of the Bill 14 s.14 objection window without valid objection.

(2) From commencement, the principles in Parts 1 to 7 are operative, the registers are maintained as derived projections, and the indexed gates are enforced under [2026] REALM-SI 5 (each fail-closed gate carrying the audited break-glass), never punitively, with disputes reserved to the single judiciary (Bill 14 s.14; CASE-LAW s.6).
