# Standing Committee Referral: Agent Loop (Harness and Determinism) Act

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Referring ministry:** Ministry of Justice policy arm
**Policy briefing:** `Judicature/ministry-of-justice/policy/2026-06-08-agent-loop-harness-and-determinism.md`
**Privy Council request:** `Judicature/requests/2026-06-08-agent-loop-harness-and-determinism-privy-council-reference.md`
**Requested output:** Agent Loop (Harness and Determinism) Act 2026, Bill 31
**Status:** research and drafting referral; the resulting Bill is law only if enacted by Royal Assent

## Referral

The Committee is asked to correct and draft Bill 31 as a no-new-law consolidation of the agent loop. The Founder has objected that the previous general consolidation title did not itself force the agent-loop process and therefore repeated the failure the Act was meant to prevent.

The corrected Bill must make the loop visible on the face of the statute: preloop plan with legal evidence, cdd-first deterministic route where available, prehook court and subagent forecast, answer or act, posthook validity review, and self-referral question unless an exempt route is recorded.

## Research Scope

The Committee reviewed:

- Acts 1 to 30 in `Legislature/legislature/bills/`;
- statutory instruments 1 to 11 in `Legislature/statutes/instruments/`;
- [2026] REALM-SI 8, [2026] REALM-SI 10, and [2026] REALM-SI 11 as the principal hook sources;
- central Privy Council, Court of Appeal, and Supreme Court judgments in `Judicature/.justice/judgments/`;
- the central citator, citation map, Gazette corpus, and cdd law retrieval outputs;
- Community Record Supreme Court materials in `Judicature/community/caselaw/2026/`;
- Ministry of Justice policy notes and public requests dated 2026-06-07 and 2026-06-08.

No committed High Court Division judgment file was located during this research. High Court Division machinery remains present in the Acts, VPR, and citation map.

## Research Findings

1. The agent-loop rule exists but is scattered across SI 8, SI 10, SI 11, public-release law, source-of-force case law, and the ministry policy route.
2. The scattered law already requires retrieval-first legal evidence, role separation or substitute checks, pre-answer law research, post-answer validity review, and source-of-authority recording for governed load-bearing work.
3. The missing clarity is public form: future agents need one place saying "preloop, answer, posthook" and "use cdd where available".
4. Any prehook question should state likely court route and subagent use before the user answers, so routing and separation are not discovered after the act.
5. Any posthook should ask whether Lexby should self-refer, unless a recorded posthook exemption applies.
6. The framework must not enact a new substantive rule. It should consolidate the route and preserve all source statuses.

## Drafting Instruction

The Clerk-Drafter is instructed to prepare Bill 31 with:

- the corrected title Agent Loop (Harness and Determinism) Act 2026;
- a no-new-law certificate;
- a source-map and source-equivalence table;
- a default agent loop sequence;
- a cdd-first deterministic CLI rule;
- a preloop plan with legal evidence;
- a prehook court-route and subagent-use forecast;
- answer or act within authority;
- posthook validity review;
- a default self-referral question;
- narrow exempt routes at both preloop and posthook;
- savings for current law, SIs, judgments, private facts, and public-release controls.
