# Statutes

The realm's legislative archive: the **enacting instruments** (Acts as passed), in chronological order, distinct from
the **consolidated** statute book. On the UK model:

- **[`../CASE-LAW.md`](../CASE-LAW.md)** is the one **consolidated, authoritative, current** statute book (the revised
  statutes). It is the single source of truth (s. 9 - no competing statute book). When in doubt, CASE-LAW.md governs.
- **`statutes/`** (here) is the **chronological record of enacting instruments** - each Act/amendment as it was passed,
  by whichever lawful route (a Parliament bill under s. 2, or a Supreme Court enactment by judgment under s. 9). It is
  the legislative history; it does not override the consolidation.

This separation keeps a single source of truth (the consolidated book) while preserving an auditable enacting record,
and never creates a second governing statute book (s. 9, s. 21, s. 22).
