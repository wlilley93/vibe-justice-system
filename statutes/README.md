# Statutes - the Acts of the Realm (enacting archive)

The realm's **enacted statute**: the Acts as passed, in order, each with its status on the UK model. With the
founding legislative programme (the first 25 Bills, drafted by the Standing Committee and given Royal Assent by the
Sovereign Founder), **the Acts are now the supreme law of the realm**.

- **The Acts of the Realm** (here) are the supreme enacted statute. An Act, on Royal Assent, is recorded here and
  comes into force per its commencement provision.
- **[`../CASE-LAW.md`](../CASE-LAW.md)** is the **founding case-law settlement** (formerly SPEC-LAW), now
  **subordinate** to the Acts: where an Act and the case law conflict, the Act prevails. The case law continues to
  govern where statute is silent, and the courts develop it in the gaps.

The Interpretation Act 2026 (Bill 15) gives effect to this transition; the Commencement and Transitional Provisions
Act 2026 (Bill 25) preserves validly-made prior rulings and saves the founding settlement where no Act displaces it.

## Register of Acts (the first 25 - IN FORCE, Royal Assent 2026-06-06)

Drafted by the Standing Committee; **Royal Assent granted by the Sovereign Founder on 2026-06-06**, so all 25 Acts

## Case-law-origin articles (the founding settlement, now codified by Act where noted)

These articles were settled by judgment into the founding case law (formerly SPEC-LAW) before the statute book
existed. The Acts now codify or supersede several of them (e.g. the court geography by the Judicature Act; the
citation scheme by the Neutral Citations Act).
