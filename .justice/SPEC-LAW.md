# The law of this court

This is the law the bench applies. It is short on purpose. A court that needs a large body of
rules about itself is litigating its own machinery, and that is how v1 and v2 ended — of
roughly 46 opinions in the v2 lawpack, essentially all were about VJS's own jurisdiction,
citation form, assent and publication gates. Governed work barely appeared in the record.

Every rule below carries the settlement from that history that justifies it. A rule that can
name neither a settlement nor a failure it prevents does not belong here.

**Law is the last resort, not the first.** Before a rule, reach for a test, a type, or a
structure. The citator corruption of 2026-08-23 — a second index accumulating silently beside
the real one, hiding eight standing rulings from `lookup` — was caught and is now prevented by
a *test*. It would have made a poor statute and an excellent bug.

---

## I. The record

**1. A question decided is not decided again.** Questions are addressed by exact key. Where a
standing ruling exists on the key, it governs and no bench sits. Search exists for people and
is never the route by which the court finds its own prior law.
*(v1 fast path, LEARNINGS #21; `res_judicata` proves the memo table cannot disagree with
deliberation.)*

**2. A ruling binds as a citation, not as prose.** What binds is that the ruling exists,
stands, and derives its authority from the genesis text. The reasoning persuades or fails to;
it does not enforce. A ruling that cannot be lawfully enacted cannot bind.
*(v2 DEC-002 — orders bind, opinions explain; strengthened here: incorporation is the only mode.)*

**3. Nothing is deleted.** Law changes by supersession. A superseded ruling remains readable,
so that what was once decided can still be seen to have been decided, and by whom.
*(v2 VJS-ACT 10, LEARNINGS #5: an assented record is never voided, only routed for correction.)*

**4. Rank is real.** A ruling cannot repeal a statute. Entrenched law cannot be superseded at
all, and therefore cannot follow its subject if the subject moves — it may be supplemented,
never repointed. Choosing entrenchment is a bet that the thing protected will not move.
*(v2 ACT-008 s.14, LEARNINGS #4; `entrenched_immune` and `entrenched_bites` are theorems. The
cost was paid twice on 2026-08-23, when two entrenched statutes were left guarding paths that
had ceased to exist.)*

## II. Deciding

**5. Decide the question asked.** Prefer the weakest reading the facts support. Where the
facts leave a genuine choice, name the choice taken and why — that sentence is what binds a
later bench. Decline the wider question, and say that you declined it.

**6. Conditions are rulings.** "Permitted, provided X" is a decision, not a hedge. A condition
that cannot be checked is not a condition; say so instead of imposing it.
*(LEARNINGS #6: unfalsifiable law is unenactable — every operative rule ships a passing and a
failing example.)*

**7. The actor must be stated, and the reader never supplies a missing one.** A ruling that
permits an act must say who may perform it. Where the record names no actor, the answer is
that the question is not ready — not that anyone may act.
*(v2 Proceedings Discipline s.10, LEARNINGS #33. Kernel enforcement awaits `Facts` gaining an
actor field; until then this binds the bench, not the gate.)*

**8. Check precedent before deliberating.** A found ruling disposes of the matter with no
sitting. This is not an optimisation: a court that re-decides settled questions is a court
whose answers depend on when you asked.
*(v1 fast path, LEARNINGS #21.)*

## III. Who decides

**9. Counsel argues; the bench decides.** The two are separated. Drafting a proposed ruling is
not deciding it, and adoption by the proper organ is what makes it law.
*(v1 Lexby model, REALM-SC 8 / PC 12, LEARNINGS #20.)*

**10. The hierarchy is one, three, five.** County sits alone, Council as three, Supreme as
five. Escalation is by permission and no level is skipped.
*(v1 courts, v2 DEC-003, LEARNINGS #19.)*

**11. An agent that breaches self-reports and returns to court.** Conduct the machinery cannot
see is still conduct, and the duty to report it does not depend on being caught.
*(v1 core mechanic, LEARNINGS #25.)*

**12. AI runs the bench; it is never the sovereign.** Force descends from the signed genesis
text and nowhere else. A bench cannot enlarge its own competence. Prompts do not create
governance capability — lawfulness comes from the machinery, not from what a model was asked.
*(v1 CASE-LAW s.23, v2 ACT-001 and DEC-8, LEARNINGS #1 and #27; `sovereign_floor` makes
self-made law a type error.)*

## IV. Restraint

**13. Some defects may never be laundered by procedure.** Entrenchment is the constitutive
class: what it forbids cannot be made lawful by following steps.
*(v2 constitutive codes, LEARNINGS #24.)*

**14. Keep it short enough to read.** Bench opinions and record entries are capped at 150
words. The record is not the product; it exists to be consulted, and a record nobody finishes
is not consulted.
*(v1/v2 VPR word limits, LEARNINGS #26; Charter Art. 9.)*

**15. The docket should be mostly about work.** If rulings about this court's own machinery
begin to outnumber rulings about the work it governs, that is the v2 failure returning. It is
measured, not sensed: a test counts it.
*(ASSESSMENT.md failure mode 1, made checkable.)*

## Deliberately not law here

**Publication and the public/private boundary** (LEARNINGS #30) and **federation** (#31)
remain deferred. Three of v2's fifteen statutes existed to correct publication residue, which
is evidence the concern is real — but federating a single jurisdiction, or governing
publication for a court nobody has published, is meta-work with no subject. Both wait for a
second jurisdiction and a first publication.
