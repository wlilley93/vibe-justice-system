## Question
How is 'completed at least 2 audits' interpreted?

## Facts
{"chosen":"auditsCompleted is a lifetime cumulative count of audits the reviewer has completed","options":["auditsCompleted is a lifetime cumulative count of audits the reviewer has completed","auditsCompleted is a count restricted to some recent window (e.g. current year)"]}

## Ruling
'Completed at least 2 audits' is satisfied when the reviewer's lifetime cumulative count of completed audits is 2 or more. `auditsCompleted` is read as a lifetime cumulative total, with no recency window. The threshold is a floor, not a rate: audits completed at any time count, and the count never resets.

## Reasoning
The record leaves a genuine choice between a lifetime count and a count restricted to a recent window. I take the lifetime reading, because it is the weakest reading the facts support: a window is an additional limitation on eligibility, and nothing in the recorded facts fixes its length or start. Reading in a window would require the court to supply a period the record does not contain, which is making law rather than applying it. I decide only the meaning of the threshold on these facts. I decline the wider questions of what counts as an audit being 'completed', and whether some future instrument may impose a recency window — a later instrument that states a window would govern its own terms, and this ruling does not forbid one.
