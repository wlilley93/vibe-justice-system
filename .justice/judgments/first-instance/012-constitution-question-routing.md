## Question
When an agent cannot proceed without an answer, which questions belong to a person and which to the court?

## Facts
{"observed":"an estate routes every such question to a person as a card; the person answers from what they know on the day, and nothing records which policy version they answered under","temporal":"a policy is versioned and [2026] VJS 11 requires a ruling applying one to cite the version in force at the time of the facts; a person asked six weeks later answers from the current version and cannot recover the earlier one","example_law":"may this invoice be sent before payor KYC is complete, where a later policy version added a KYC step","example_fact":"which entity is the counterparty on this deal","cost":"a bench sitting costs; a card costs a person an interruption and yields no citable record"}

## Ruling
A question belongs to the court when its answer is produced by applying a versioned instrument to facts already on the record — the KYC-before-invoice example. It belongs to a person when it supplies a fact the record lacks, or exercises a discretion the instrument leaves open — the counterparty example. The test is versionability: if answering requires naming a policy version, it is the court's; if answering requires knowing the world, it is the person's. A person's answer to a law question does not bind and may not be cited as one, because it carries no version. Where a question mixes both, the person supplies the fact first and the court then decides on the completed record. Routing either way is permitted, provided the routing step records which branch it took, and court answers record the version cited.

## Reasoning
The facts leave a genuine choice over questions that are neither pure fact nor pure application — discretion an instrument leaves open. I placed those with the person, because nothing recorded shows the instrument constrains the choice, and a court that absorbs every blocking question becomes a docket about itself (r.15). The dividing line is versionability, not difficulty or cost: [2026] VJS 11 makes the version in force at the time of the facts part of any ruling applying a policy, and a person asked six weeks later cannot recover it, so a card cannot produce a valid answer to a law question however well the person knows the work. I decline the wider questions and say so: whether card answers already given are void, and how the estate must build the routing step. The record names no router; the classification binds whoever the estate names.
