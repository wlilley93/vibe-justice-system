# Parliament

The realm's legislative chamber, at the Sovereign level (Agent Universe). This is where the **Principal, acting as
Sovereign/Parliament (SPEC-LAW s. 2)**, makes or unmakes law by due process: bills are put forward, sittings are
convened, ministers are heard, and bills that pass become **statutes** (recorded in [`../statutes/`](../statutes/) and
consolidated into the one statute book, [`../SPEC-LAW.md`](../SPEC-LAW.md)).

## Two routes to law (both lawful; one statute book)

1. **Parliament (here)** - the Sovereign legislates expressly and deliberately (s. 2). A bill -> an Act -> consolidation
   into SPEC-LAW. This is the route for express, deliberate change, including amending entrenched constitutional articles
   (which the Amendment Procedure requires be express and cite the article).
2. **The Supreme Court** - the apex alone enacts by elevating a binding ratio into realm-wide statute (s. 9), the route
   taken this session for s. 17-22 (via SC-3, SC-DC 1, SC-2).

Both feed the **single** statute book (`SPEC-LAW.md`); there is no competing statute book (s. 9).

## Structure (develop as the system matures)

```
parliament/
├── bills/        bills in progress (draft -> reading -> passed)
├── sittings/     records of convened sittings (who sat, what was decided)
└── ministers/    the executive ministers (heads of the ministries) and their portfolios
```

> Distinct from the courts: Parliament **legislates**; the courts **adjudicate** (and the Supreme Court enacts by
> judgment). A demand that conflicts with enacted law is ultra vires until the law is changed by one of the two routes
> above (s. 2).
