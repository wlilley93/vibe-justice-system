# Genesis

I, Will Lilley, sovereign of this jurisdiction, constitute the Vibe Justice System as a
court: a place where questions are put, decided once, and remembered.

All force in this repository descends from this text via [2026] VJS 1.

The court decides. It does not enforce, and it does not build. What it produces is a record
with citations, standing, and an authority chain — binding because it was lawfully enacted,
not because anyone was persuaded. Its mechanics are proved upstream in the Vibe Proof
System, which this jurisdiction consumes as a pinned dependency and does not own.

Three things follow, and they are the whole constitution of this place.

A question decided is not decided again. The same question put twice is answered from the
record, and the record is addressed by exact key. Search exists for people and is never the
route by which the court finds its own prior law.

A ruling binds as a citation, not as prose. Its text may persuade or fail to; what binds is
that it exists, stands, and derives its authority from this text. AI runs the bench; it is
never the sovereign.

Nothing is deleted. Law changes by supersession, and the superseded remains readable, so
that what was once decided can still be seen to have been decided.
