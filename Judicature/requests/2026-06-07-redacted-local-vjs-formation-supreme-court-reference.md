# Redacted Supreme Court Reference: Local VJS Formation, Data Boundary, and Residual Points of Law

**Date:** 2026-06-07  
**Proposed court:** Supreme Court of the Vibe Justice System  
**Route requested:** conditional / stayed apex reference after Court of Appeal review  
**Status:** public request; system-data only; not a judgment or order

## Data Boundary

This reference deliberately omits the local repo name, local paths, screenshots, logs, operational facts, client/project identifiers, hostnames, credentials, and unredacted evidence.

The unredacted evidential material remains in the proper private/local record. The Supreme Court is asked to rule on the public-law questions without requiring protected facts to be placed in the central public record unless a competent legal route requires a redacted or anonymised disclosure.

## Procedural Posture

A local jurisdiction-repo formation and regularisation question has arisen. The Principal first requested Supreme Court treatment, then directed that the matter should go through the appellate route first to test the first-instance approach.

The immediate route is therefore Court of Appeal review of the local first-instance approach and any proposed interim preservation order. This Supreme Court reference is preserved for residual apex questions that remain after that appellate review.

## Authorities Already Identified

The Supreme Court should treat the following as already bearing on the reference:

1. **VPR 2** - binding ratio may dispose of points by fast-path citation.
2. **VPR 3** - ordinary progression is First Instance, Court of Appeal, then Supreme Court, absent an express leapfrog certificate.
3. **VPR 9** - self-appeal is required before irreversible implementation; reversible provisional preservation steps may proceed if candidly marked.
4. **`[2026] REALM-SC 6`** - operational jurisdiction-repos may carry local first-instance hearing-centres of the one judiciary.
5. **`[2026] REALM-SC 7`** - validity is assessed entry by entry; no wholesale validation, wholesale voiding, or retroactive fiction.
6. **`[2026] REALM-SC 8`** - Lexby-authored drafts have no legal force unless adopted by the competent organ.
7. **`[2026] REALM-PC 14`** - local court formation depends on substance, seat, and conformance, not file presence alone.
8. **`[2026] REALM-PC 15`** - the superrepo is the realm-as-state, not itself a local County Court.
9. **`[2026] REALM-PC 17`** - local/private jurisdiction records and canonical/public records are distinct.

## Questions for the Supreme Court

The questions preserved for apex decision are:

1. What minimum redacted/system-data case file may the Supreme Court receive when the underlying dispute concerns a local jurisdiction-repo and the evidence contains private or operational facts?
2. May a central apex reference identify only the abstract legal questions and the existence of a private/local evidence record, leaving unredacted evidence outside the public central record?
3. What public pointer, if any, may central Judicature hold to private/local evidence without disclosing a protected fact?
4. Do `[2026] REALM-PC 14`, `[2026] REALM-PC 15`, `[2026] REALM-PC 17`, and `[2026] REALM-SC 7` fully answer local formation/regularisation disputes, or do they leave residual apex guidance needed on incomplete local court records?
5. What is the correct appellate process for reviewing an interim preservation order where the order text was drafted by Lexby but must derive force only from adoption by a properly constituted court?
6. When the proper factual record is private/local, what may the Court of Appeal certify onward to the Supreme Court?

## Requested Order or Direction

The Supreme Court is asked, after Court of Appeal review, to:

1. confirm the public/private data boundary for apex references involving local jurisdiction-repos;
2. state the minimum public case-file contents required for a valid redacted reference;
3. confirm whether existing authorities dispose of the formation and regularisation points by fast-path citation;
4. remit implementation to the proper local first-instance court where the dispute is factual or restorative rather than foundational;
5. give procedural guidance on how interim preservation orders are to be sought, reviewed, and, if necessary, appealed without leaking protected repo-level facts into the public central record.

## Limits

This request does not ask the Supreme Court to decide private facts on the public record. It asks for legal guidance about route, data boundary, minimum case-file content, and residual apex questions.
