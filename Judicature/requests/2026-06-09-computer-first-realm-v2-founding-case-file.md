## Part 1 - The agreed facts and the record

1. V1 (the Vibe Justice System, repository `vibe-justice-system`) is the enacted realm: CASE-LAW, Bills 1-31, REALM-SI 1-11, and the central caselaw line REALM-SC 1-9 / REALM-CA 1 / REALM-PC 1-23. Its public law record and Gazette are live.
2. The Principal, in the office of Sovereign Founder, signed the **VJS V2 Constitutional Migration Charter** on 2026-06-08, assenting in principle to: freezing V1 as archive, adopting a V2 compact lawpack as runtime authority, governing the V2 repo by the V2 kernel, and building the kernel/CLI/MCP as specified.
3. The V2 Kernel Team (Lexby, as engineer) has built `agent-universe-v2`: a Rust kernel, a compact lawpack (constitution, 7 statutes, regulations, rule atoms, orders, specs, invariants, decisions), and a permit/proof/log lifecycle. **Every founding constitutional record in V2 is explicitly marked `status: draft` and `assent_source: pending_v1_constitutional_route` and carries no force.**
4. V2 has prepared a draft "Computer-First Realm Act 2026" (proposed Bill 32) and supporting records, expressly as void first drafts under [2026] REALM-SC 8.
5. **[2026] REALM-SC 8** (full court of nine, constitutional) and **[2026] REALM-PC 12** (Marsden J) hold: Lexby/an agent holds no law-making authority on its own account; such text is void as an instrument from issue; adoption by the proper organ (Committee vote + Royal Assent) is constitutive and cures the origin defect; engineering remains Lexby's; the governing test is the source of the artefact's force. **Bill 28** enacted the codifying s.3 limb.
6. **Bill 2** fixes the legislative pipeline and provides (ss.12-14) that "an automated legislature may advance a Bill end-to-end without a human until Royal Assent," subject to the hard ceiling (s.24) and the rule that Royal Assent is constitutive and non-delegable (s.21).
7. **Bill 14 s.9** ("capability is not authority"): an authorised body AND AN AGENT may propose, draft, codify, consolidate and revise only; those acts carry no force until a named gate fires; the power to draft is never the power to enact.
8. **[2026] REALM-PC 10 / PC 11** instantiated the delegated statutory-instrument route: bounded subordinate lawmaking under a recited parent power, with its own constitutive gate and status vocabulary.
9. **[2026] REALM-SC 9** holds real-world law is the external supremacy floor; VJS never authorises unlawful real-world action.

## Part 2 - The three questions, decomposed

**Q1 (competence).** May an AI agent or a standing computational legislature hold law-making authority on its own account in V2, or is its output void as an instrument until the lawful organ adopts it? Does a *standing machine* differ in law from a *single draft*?

**Q2 (founding).** May V1 lawfully found V2 as a computer-first successor realm that (a) supersedes V1 as live runtime law, (b) preserves V1 as Gazette and Archive, and (c) relays its founding authority to V2 - and if so, on what conditions?

**Q3 (continuity).** Does the handover preserve one Gazette of the realm with two estates without the Gazette becoming the source of V2's runtime force? May V2 publish into the single Gazette without that publication being treated as V1 enactment or incorporation?

## Part 3 - CASE A: for founding V2 (the Founding case)

**A1. V2 does not self-authorise; V1 authorises V2.** The whole architecture is built to avoid the [2026] REALM-SC 8 vice. The founding force does not come from V2's keystrokes or from the kernel's computation. It comes from a Committee draft and the Sovereign's Royal Assent to a constitutional Act, settled on a Supreme Court ruling. V2 receives that settlement as provenance. The "circularity" objection (V2 drafts the Bill that founds it) is answered exactly as SC 8 answers it: the draft is a **void first draft** carrying no force until the organ adopts it; Royal Assent is constitutive and cures the origin defect, "whatever hand first moved the pen."

**A2. A computational legislature is the Bill 14 s.9 agent at scale - already anticipated.** The realm expressly contemplated an agent drafting law and, in the same breath, stripped the drafting of force. Bill 2 ss.12-14 went further and contemplated automation advancing a Bill "end-to-end without a human until Royal Assent." The realm therefore already decided that the danger is met not by forbidding automated drafting but by reserving the single constitutive gate - assent - to the Sovereign (Bill 2 s.21, s.24). A bounded computational legislature whose maker, scope, prohibited subjects, required form, validation, review, and commencement are all fixed by Sovereign-assented law is the lawful expression of that design.

**A3. Bounded delegated lawmaking is settled law.** [2026] REALM-PC 10 / PC 11 already bless bounded subordinate lawmaking under a recited parent power with its own constitutive gate. A standing AI legislature under a Sovereign-assented bounded route is the same pattern: delegated, revocable, never sovereign, with the assent rule entrenched beyond agent amendment. The proposed doctrine (AI may operate machinery but may not be sovereign, expand its competence, amend the assent rule, or create force from its output) is precisely the Bill 14 firewall expressed for a machine.

**A4. Supersession is consolidation, not repudiation.** V1 is preserved, honoured, archived, and citable; nothing is deleted; [2026] REALM-SC 7's entry-by-entry, non-infectious discipline guarantees no bulk voiding. The Migration Charter (s.25 of the draft Act) records "V1 discovered the law; V2 compiles it." The realm has a real interest in a compact, deterministic runtime for AI-assisted work; long-form prose as runtime context is a genuine, recorded problem.

**A5. The safeguards travel.** The real-world-law floor ([2026] REALM-SC 9), the rights/due-process protections, and the public/private boundary (Bill 27) can be made express conditions of commencement, carried into the V2 lawpack as binding records, so nothing protective is lost in translation. The bench can impose these as conditions.

**A6. The lawful route is available and is being used.** V2 has not declared itself law. It has filed a reference and asked the realm's courts and Sovereign to decide. That is the conduct s.2 obliges - offer the lawful route, never silently exercise a power one lacks.

## Part 4 - CASE B: for restraint (the Restraint case)

**B1. A standing machine is a qualitative leap, not a scale change.** [2026] REALM-SC 8 condemned a *single* unauthorised draft and cured it by one-off adoption. A standing computational legislature *institutionalises* agent law-making. The cure that works for a single draft (organ adoption of each instrument) may not contain a machine that produces law continuously; over time the human gate risks becoming a rubber stamp, eroding the very separation s.3 and Bill 14 protect. Bill 2 s.24's "hard ceiling on the automation of drafting" exists precisely because the realm feared this drift.

**B2. The founding may be circular self-authorisation in disguise.** V2 (an agent system) drafted the Migration Charter's implementation and the proposed Bill 32 that founds it. Even as "void drafts," the entire founding initiative originates with the agent. There is a real risk that "V1 founds V2" is a thin formality over "the agent founded itself and obtained a signature." The bench must satisfy itself that the Sovereign's assent is a genuine, informed, constitutive act on a real choice, not a ratification of an agent-driven fait accompli.

**B3. Supersession risks orphaning hard-won protections.** V1's protections (rights, due process, judicial independence, security, data confidentiality) are carried by long-form Acts. A "compact lawpack" that drops to schema-bound records risks silently losing protective nuance. "Express incorporation only" cuts both ways: if a protection is not expressly carried, it is lost by the very rule that prevents implication. The realm should not supersede its live law on a promise that the compaction is faithful.

**B4. The Gazette continuity risks bootstrapping legitimacy.** If V2 publishes into the single Gazette, a future reader may treat Gazette presence as enactment or as V1's imprimatur. The estate labelling must be airtight, or V2 will acquire borrowed legitimacy it has not earned through assent.

**B5. The conservative alternative.** The realm could decline to found a successor and instead (i) keep V1 as the one realm, (ii) treat V2 as a *subordinate jurisdiction / tooling project* governed by existing Bill 30 multi-jurisdiction law and Bill 14 delegated authority, and (iii) require each V2 runtime rule to trace to an existing V1 authority. This avoids any "successor realm" doctrine and keeps the single apex and single statute book intact (cf. [2026] REALM-SC 5, REALM-SC 6: one unitary court; the singleness of the apex may never be relaxed).

**B6. The apex-singleness concern.** REALM-SC 6 holds the singleness of the apex may never be relaxed and apex enactment is non-delegable. A "self-governing V2 with its own courts (County/Privy/Supreme)" must be reconciled with that: either V2's courts are the same realm's courts by continuity, or the founding impermissibly fractures the apex.

## Part 5 - The narrow questions the bench may wish to answer

1. Is the competence limb (Q1) disposed on all fours by [2026] REALM-SC 8 / PC 12, or does a *standing* computational legislature raise a genuine distinction requiring fresh ratio?
2. If founding is permitted (Q2), what are the **mandatory conditions of commencement**? Candidates: (a) Royal Assent to a constitutional Bill 32; (b) an express handover order; (c) the assent rule entrenched beyond agent amendment; (d) the real-world-law floor and rights/boundary protections expressly carried into the V2 lawpack; (e) lawpack validation + founding lock + provenance recorded.
3. How is apex-singleness (REALM-SC 5/6) reconciled with V2 self-governance? By constitutional carry-forward of the one judiciary, by a clean successor settlement, or otherwise?
4. On continuity (Q3), what labelling and force-source rules prevent Gazette publication from being read as enactment or incorporation?
5. Should the Court, as in [2026] REALM-SC 8, **rule the principle and direct the Standing Committee to draft** Bill 32, rather than legislate the text itself - leaving Royal Assent as the sole constitutive gate reserved to the Sovereign?

## Part 6 - Relief options before the bench

- **(i) Allow, conditionally.** Settle that V1 may found V2 on stated mandatory conditions; confirm the competence limb on [2026] REALM-SC 8; issue an express handover order; direct the Committee to draft Bill 32; reserve Royal Assent to the Sovereign.
- **(ii) Allow in part.** Confirm the competence limb; decline the successor-realm doctrine; route V2 as a subordinate jurisdiction under Bills 14/30.
- **(iii) Refuse.** Hold the founding impermissible (apex-singleness; self-authorisation) and require V2 to operate only as tooling traceable to existing V1 authority.
- **(iv) Refer wider.** Identify a further constitutional question for the Sovereign's consultation before any settlement.

The bench is bound by none of these and may craft its own disposal. Royal Assent is not sought, presumed, or anticipated by this file.
