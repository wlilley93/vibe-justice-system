# [2026] REALM-SC 5

> Reconstituted into the provenance scheme from the previous-system source `lexby-legacy-benchmark-sc-dc-1-divisions-over-one-spine.md`. This is a condensed record; the full original judgment text is retained at the source. Enacts s. 21.

## Matter
Should the realm be re-organised into domain-specific courts, or remain one unitary court with domain divisions over one spine?

## Ratio
One global CASE-LAW applied jurisdiction-locally by one apex with no competing sovereigns permits the single Vibe Justice System court to be organised into domain DIVISIONS over one procedural spine, but FORBIDS its fracture into domain COURTS that would own parallel substantive law, statutes, citators, or apexes (s. 9). A product feature producing client-facing legal advice is outside VJS governance even where it borrows the deliberation pattern, and must be severed from the governance citation series, bench, and pseudonyms (s. 14). Entrenched by the declaratory ordinary article s. 21; disposed on the fast-path (s. 11(c)).
