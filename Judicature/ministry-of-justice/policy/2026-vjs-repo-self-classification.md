# Policy briefing: the self-classification of the VJS repository (is it a County Court?)

**Type:** Ministry policy briefing (policy-arm: Ministry policy -> court referral / Standing Committee drafting,
Bill 29)
**To:** the Ministry of Justice (MoJ), governance ministry of the Judicature
**From:** the Prime Minister (the Founder, acting in the executive office, CASE-LAW s.2)
**Subject:** by the realm's own logic, is the VJS repository a County Court repo, or something else?
**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Status:** the settled core is recorded; the open refinement is referred to the Privy Council ([2026] REALM-PC 15)

> A policy briefing, not an instrument of law. The MoJ records the settled position (citator fast-path,
> CASE-LAW s.11(c)) and refers the genuine first-impression refinement to the court before any drafting
> (Bill 29 s.13A(3)). Authorship of a briefing is the advocate/advisor function (CASE-LAW s.3), not law-making.

## 1. The question

The Founder asks whether the VJS repository is, by the realm's own logic, a County Court repo.

## 2. The settled core (cite and proceed, no fresh fork)

It is not. By CASE-LAW **s.22(3)** (the realm-as-state, enacted by [2026] REALM-SC 6), the VJS repository is the
realm's **super-repo** (the "Agent Universe", now VJS): the administrative container organised over a Ministry
of Justice (governance only, pure and meta: CASE-LAW, the VPR, and the apex courts) and the executive ministries
holding the operational jurisdiction-repos. The distinction from a County Court is categorical and settled:

- A **County Court** (s.22(1)) is a LOCAL first-instance hearing-centre, one per jurisdiction-repo, that **owns
  no statute book, no apex, and no separate citation series**, records jurisdiction-local caselaw on a
  `CC-<repo>` series only, and **may never enact realm-wide statute**.
- The **VJS repo** owns the whole statute book (CASE-LAW + the Acts + the SI register), hosts the single central
  apex (the Court of Appeal and Supreme Court, s.22(2)) and the Privy Council, and carries the central `REALM-*`
  citation series. It has exactly what a County Court is forbidden to have.

On the [2026] REALM-PC 14 formation test, the VJS repo **fails the "seat" condition** for a County Court (which
requires recording jurisdiction-local precedent and owning no forked statute book or apex), and Sumberly J's
obiter in that ruling confirms a `.justice/` directory alone never constitutes a County Court (substance and seat
govern). Section 22(3) is express that the super-repo "is not a sovereign, **a court**, or a second governance
home": the courts sit within it; it is not itself a court, still less a County Court. The County Courts are the
operational jurisdiction-repos under the Executive (e.g. `CC-ACMECO` under MBES). The VJS repo is the realm above
them all.

## 3. The genuine first-impression refinement (referred to the court)

One question is genuinely first-impression and unsettled, and the MoJ refers it to the Privy Council before any
drafting (Bill 29 s.13A(3)): **s.22 distributes the first-instance tier as County Courts "one per
jurisdiction-repo," yet the realm operates a central Privy Council (`REALM-PC`) for its own constitutional
first-instance matters.** Where are first-instance matters concerning the super-repo *itself* (the realm's own
governance and engineering) heard, and what is the status of the central Privy Council relative to the
distributed s.22(1) County Courts: a distinct central constitutional first-instance court, or the "County Court
at the super-repo"? The realm has operated the Privy Council as the central constitutional first instance by
practice and by the Bill 16 citation scheme, but no ruling has settled its relationship to the distributed
County Courts or the super-repo's own first-instance seat.

## 4. Recommendation

The MoJ recommends:
1. **Record the settled core** (sections 2): the VJS repo is the realm-as-state super-repo, not a County Court
   (s.22(3); REALM-SC 6). No court or Committee action is needed on the core; it is followed and cited.
2. **Refer the refinement** (section 3) to the **Privy Council** ([2026] REALM-PC 15) for a clarifying ruling on
   the super-repo's self-classification and the Privy Council's status, so the realm's self-understanding is on
   the record.
3. If the ruling identifies a need to make the position clear on the face of the statute book, the Standing
   Committee may draft a short declaratory conforming note to CASE-LAW s.22 (express deliberate amendment); the
   ruling itself otherwise stands as declaratory precedent.

## 5. Referral

The MoJ refers the section 3 refinement to the Privy Council. The settled core is recorded and requires no
referral.
