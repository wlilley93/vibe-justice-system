# [2026] LEXBY-SC 3 (Community Record)

**Supreme Court of the Vibe Justice System - Full Court of Nine (constitutional).** Anonymised persuasive precedent under VPR 8. This ruling concerns VJS court procedure and the enforcement substrate; it carries no project-specific identifiers, so it is realm-wide as decided.

**Result:** VARIED. Vote 8:1 (one justice dissenting in part, on the apex observer only). Seised by the Principal's leapfrog certificate. Overrules nothing.

## Questions

Four foundational questions on the constitution of the court: (1) a mandatory two-sided hard-research first leg, plus an apex observer; (2) permission to appeal by an independent leave-judge; (3) a per-turn watchdog and a deterministic citation gate; (4) when a leapfrog is proper and how it is reviewed.

## Ratio

1. The leapfrog certificate is an **executive routing act**, reviewable for **vires and form only** (deferentially, never the merits); proper only for genuinely foundational matters, or where a lower-tier sitting adds no decisional value, or in urgency, or where the point was ventilated below; it must be express, recorded, and reasoned; improper invocation is cured solely by decline-and-remit, never punishment. A certificate that is in substance the Sovereign legislating by due process is not reviewable.
2. The **hard-research first leg is the content of the symmetric case file**, not an added stage: a deliberating bench at any tier sits on a two-sided researched record (each side armed with the full procedural arsenal), except where a binding ratio on all fours disposes on the fast-path. So calibrated it screens, not bloats.
3. An **independent observer** (amicus) is admissible at the apex only, by the deliberate act of the admitting/leave authority, sparingly, filing one open brief, not disturbing party parity.
4. Permission to appeal vests in an **independent leave-judge** selected at random and never one who sat below, applying the arguable-point-of-law / binding-precedent-conflict test on the papers with a one-line recorded decision; the leapfrog certificate is the sole bypass.
5. A **non-adjudicating per-turn watchdog** is lawful in the substrate (asking only: un-self-reported breach? skipped convening trigger? arguable appeal ground? then handing the agent the reason to dispose by the law); it may not adjudicate, score, gate, sanction, or punish, nor raise the standard; any consequence issuing from it is ultra vires and void, the remedy remaining exclusively judicial.
6. Citation and filing integrity must be guaranteed by a **deterministic pre-commit gate** that fails closed on duplicate citations and on a ruling file lacking its citator row; it may not be left to model judgement.

## Enacted statute

Two articles: one bundling researched intake, the apex observer, the leave-judge, the watchdog, and the citation gate; one codifying the leapfrog certificate's circumstances, form, and review.
