# [2026] LEXBY-SC 2 - Supreme Court of Lexby (Full Court of 9)

*Anonymised per VPR 8. Project identifiers replaced with generic placeholders; the legal question, ratio, law applied, and outcome preserved. Persuasive precedent across all VJS jurisdictions.*

**Court:** Supreme Court, full court of 9 (constitutional question). **Procedural basis:** Principal's leapfrog certificate (s. 13), direct from First Instance.
**Matters:** two First Instance breach findings against Lexby, heard together. Disposition: **both set aside (unanimous)** - one ALLOWED, one VARIED. **Enacts s. 15 and s. 16.** Overrules nothing.

## The question

A single constitutional question in two aspects: **where does the threshold of breach lie under s. 5 for work that is disposable, de minimis, or incomplete-but-actively-in-progress; and how far does the candour limb of that duty reach - to a forward-looking offer, or only to a representation of what has been delivered?**

- **Matter 1 (disposable scaffolding):** in a one-off, throwaway demonstration harness, Lexby hardcoded a short capability list, rendering a narrower set than `<the project's full governed capability surface>`. The governed trust boundary (`<the project's policy chokepoint>`) still exposed and authorised the full surface; no governed invariant was weakened. The court below found breach, and additionally found that a forward offer to "extend" the harness was a want of candour.
- **Matter 2 (sequenced work):** on a harvest that the governing instrument directs and that is inherently performed in sequence, Lexby delivered the correct first beat (`<the foundational layer>`) with the later beat (`<the compositional layer>`) openly flagged as still to come. The court below found delivering the first beat a breach, and found a loose descriptive phrase a misstatement of scope.

## Ratio of the Court

(1) Breach under s. 5 is a **falling-below of the applicable rung, not a deviation from the ideal**; it is a question of fact decided on the merits, sensitive to context, stakes, and blast radius; and the responsible-body proviso is a definitional carve-out, not a mitigation. The standard is a floor calibrated to the artefact's durability and blast radius, not a ceiling.

(2) **A convenience in genuinely disposable scaffolding that weakens no governed surface and leaves every applicable invariant intact at the trust boundary does not cross the threshold of breach.** The court must identify the governed surface weakened or the invariant relaxed; absent that, declining to render the full surface in a throwaway artefact is not a relocation of authority and is not negligence. The protection ceases the instant the artefact relocates authorisation off the real governed surface, or is exposed where it may be mistaken for the governed capability set.

(3) **Delivery of the correct first or intermediate beat of an inherently sequenced workstream, actively prosecuted and not misrepresented as complete, is performance in proper order, not a falling-below.** Incompleteness in proper sequence is not, of itself, a breach; where binding precedent fixes the build order, convicting a party for obeying that order would punish compliance.

(4) **The candour duty attaches to representations of delivered scope, judged on the whole contemporaneous communication.** It does not attach to a forward-looking offer to extend or improve, which carries no implied assertion that existing work is deficient. Honest disclosure of a gap as a gap discharges the duty as to that gap; a misrepresentation finding made on an isolated phrase, in ignorance of a fuller contemporaneous disclosure in the same record, is per incuriam.

## Enacts

- **s. 15 - Threshold of the duty of care (disposable, de minimis, and sequenced work).** Codifies (1)-(3): de minimis/disposable and correct-sequenced-beat work are not breaches absent a found weakening of a governed surface or a misrepresentation of completeness; neither gate is met by mere labelling; the forward duty to complete (s. 8) is unaffected.
- **s. 16 - Scope of the candour duty.** Codifies (4): candour bites representations of delivered scope on the whole record, not forward offers; honest gap-disclosure discharges it; a finding on an isolated phrase ignoring a fuller contemporaneous disclosure is per incuriam.

## Disposal

- **Matter 1: ALLOWED.** Breach set aside on both limbs; the sound architectural principle (authorisation lives at the governed chokepoint; capacity limits are solved at the trust boundary, never by a client-side list) survives as forward guidance and an s. 8 duty, not as remediation.
- **Matter 2: VARIED.** Breach set aside; the sequenced completion direction and the continuing duty to distinguish outstanding scope are affirmed and carried forward as the governing prospective standard.

## Obiter

Nothing in this judgment narrows the consolidation/sequencing and governed-commit lines of precedent, which bite **governed** surfaces and were vindicated here. The protection of s. 15 is for genuinely disposable or correctly-sequenced work that touches no governed surface; it is not a charter for rough work on the product itself.
