# Home Office

An Executive-branch ministry for the realm's internal / home affairs (personal domain). This charter is
**public** (Bill 27 public-mechanics rule): it states the Office's mechanics; any personal contents are
private (separate gitignored repos; the public VJS holds system data only).

  and the security estate (the Ministry of Data Security).
- Develops policy through its policy arm (Ministry policy -> Standing Committee drafting) where its domain needs
