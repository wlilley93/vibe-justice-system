# Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills (MBES)

The realm's executive operational ministry, in the Executive branch. Renamed from the Ministry for Business,
Work and Skills by the VJS (Constitution and Machinery) Act 2026 (Bill 27); it holds the operational
jurisdiction-repos (CASE-LAW s. 22(3); Bill 5 s. 14). This charter is **public** (Bill 27 public-mechanics
rule): it states the Ministry's mechanics; the departments' project contents are private (separate gitignored
repos).

## Mechanics (what the Ministry does)

- **Owns the refactoring suite** (Bill 27): the operative content of
  `Judicature/.justice/suites/refactoring.md`, legalised and amended as a statutory instrument under the
  Bill 26 framework.
- **Holds the operational departments** and their jurisdiction-local repos (each a County Court hearing-centre
  of the one judiciary, CASE-LAW s. 22; local precedent stays in its repo and is not in the public record).
- **Develops policy** through its policy arm (Ministry policy -> Standing Committee drafting) for engineering,
  refactoring, and skills matters.

## Departments

| Department | Role | Repos (private) |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | High Court, Engineering Division | the engineering projects (separate gitignored repos) |
| Legal | High Court, Legal Division (Chancery) | the legal-department work (private) |
| Business Operations | client / business operations | private |
| Skills and Education | skills + education | private |

## Registers it holds

| Register | Visibility | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The refactoring suite (the law) | **public** | the Refactoring Suite Instrument (Legislature/statutes/instruments/) |
| Department / project contents | **private** | the departments' own gitignored repos |

## The principle: mechanics public, project contents private

Per Bill 27: the Ministry's mechanics (this charter), and the law it owns (the refactoring suite SI), are
public. The departments' operational project repos are private (separate gitignored repos), holding the
personal/operational work. The public VJS holds system data only.
