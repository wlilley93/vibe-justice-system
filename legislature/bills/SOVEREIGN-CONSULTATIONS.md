# Sovereign Consultations - the Royal Assent docket

The first 25 Bills are drafted by the Standing Committee and **presented for Royal Assent**. Most were routed to
the Privy Council, which flagged a shared constitutional point for **Sovereign consultation** before assent. The
Founder may, on consultation, **affirm** (grant Assent) or **overturn**. The root question recurs across the docket;
the per-bill questions are below.

- **Bills drafted:** 25/25  ·  **flagged for consultation:** 22  ·  **second-round (deadlock-broken):** 2 (Bill 11, Bill 17)

## The root question (recurs on most bills)

Per Privy Council guidance, seating the enacted **Acts above the entrenched case law** (CASE-LAW ss. 1, 2, 9) is
only lawful by **express amendment**, not assertion. **Bill 1 (Acts of Union 2026)** is drafted to be that express
amendment (Schedule 1 amends ss. 1, 2, 9, 10 by number). **Affirming Bill 1 on Royal Assent resolves the root
question for the whole programme.**
