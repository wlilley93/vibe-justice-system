# Commercial Licensing - Vibe Justice System (VJS)

VJS is released under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
That license makes VJS free for noncommercial use. Commercial use requires
a separate paid license, described here.

## Do I need a commercial license?

You need a commercial license if your use of VJS has any anticipated commercial
application. In practice that includes:

- Using VJS inside a for-profit company (including on internal tools,
  products, or client work), regardless of company size.
- Using VJS as part of a paid product or service you offer to others.
- Any use on behalf of a commercial entity.

You do not need a commercial license for noncommercial use, which the
underlying license defines to include personal study, hobby projects, amateur
pursuits, research, and use by charities, educational institutions, public
research/health/safety/environmental organizations, and government institutions.

If you are unsure which side of the line your use falls on, contact us before
you deploy - we're happy to clarify, and it's far cheaper than finding out later.

## What a commercial license gives you

- The right to use VJS for commercial purposes.
