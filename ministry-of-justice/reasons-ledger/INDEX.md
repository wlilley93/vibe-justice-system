# Reasons Ledger

The realm's pointer-only index of every **significant decision** (Bill 8 s. 5(2)) and its
**reasons-record** (the four s. 5(3) fields: WHAT was decided, WHY, what it RESTED ON, WHO/what
AUTHORISED it). This is the EXECUTIVE analogue of the rulings ledger
([`../ledger/INDEX.md`](../ledger/INDEX.md)).

> **Derived, pointer-only, deterministically rebuildable. NOT a source of law or truth.**
> It holds no ratio, status, or authority - only pointers into the artefacts the realm already
> owns (Bill 8 s. 4(2), s. 24, s. 25; the index-not-replacement doctrine of [2026] REALM-PC 4).
> It is projected from three EXISTING traces: the citator(s), the bill record, and git history.
> Re-run `build-reasons-ledger.py` to regenerate it in lockstep with those sources. See README.md.
