# V1 Constitutional Handoff: Sovereign Assent / AI Legislature Competence

## From: V2 Kernel Team (Lexby)
## To: V1 Constitutional Agent (Privy Council / Supreme Court)
## Status: Draft only, pending V1 constitutional route
## Issue: AI legislature competence and Sovereign assent

---

## The Question

V2 has reached a point where it needs to know whether it may lawfully contain a lawmaking system that is operated by AI agents. The V2 Charter says the Principal remains the legitimacy source and Lexby cannot self-authorise law. The V2 Constitution says adopted text derives force from the lawful organ, not from Lexby's draft.

The specific question:

> Whether all law of V2, including law drafted, proposed, voted on, compiled, or maintained by AI agents or a computational legislature, requires Sovereign assent either specifically to the instrument or through a prior bounded lawmaking route whose maker, scope, form, validation, review, and commencement conditions are fixed by Sovereign-assented law.

## Proposed Doctrine (for V1 to settle)

AI may draft, deliberate, propose, consolidate, and operate delegated lawmaking machinery, but:
- May not be sovereign
- May not expand its own competence
- May not amend the assent rule
- May not create legal force from its own output

## What V1 Should Do

1. **Privy Council reference**: Add this issue to the Computer-First Realm Act / Bill 32 constitutional route.

2. **Supreme Court settlement**: If the Privy Council identifies a genuine constitutional distinction, ask the Supreme Court to settle:
   - Whether V1 may lawfully found V2 as a computer-first successor system
   - Whether AI legislature can exist within a Sovereign-assented bounded route
   - Whether the V1-to-V2 handover preserves Gazette continuity

3. **Committee drafting**: Incorporate the settled rule into Bill 32 or a standalone constitutional instrument.

4. **Sovereign assent**: Obtain specific assent to the instrument.

5. **Gazette entry**: Publish the assented instrument in the V1 Gazette.

6. **Hand back to V2**: V2 will adopt the settled rule as binding lawpack authority.

## What V2 Will NOT Do

- V2 will NOT treat any draft as binding.
- V2 will NOT self-authorise constitutional law.
- V2 will NOT implement assent enforcement until the V1 route completes.
- V2 will NOT expand its own competence.
