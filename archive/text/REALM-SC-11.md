# [2026] REALM-SC 11 - The server-of-law and the embeddable governance client

> Public-only record. The full judgment was decided on a confidential commercial matter; the client facts are withheld and only the public ratio is reproduced here. This is a V1-lineage judgment: persuasive in V2, never binding unless expressly incorporated.

## The question

Whether an embeddable, corporate-re-skinned client that renders the same canonical governance (the realm's law, its procedure, its bench constitution, and its duty of care) inside a host product, served from a central server of canonical law, is a lawful non-operative presentation-layer **division over the one spine**, or instead a forbidden parallel **court** that owns substantive law, or a forbidden product-as-governance feature.

## The holding

A full court (leapfrog) held, unanimous on characterisation and on disposal in result, that the line is **substance, not vocabulary**: **re-naming canonical governance is permitted; re-housing substantive law, the citator, the bench, or the apex inside the product is forbidden.** Ownership of the renderer is not ownership of the law: a vendor controls when canonical law is **displayed**, never **what** it is. Such a client is lawful only if it satisfies six cumulative conditions, each a found fact and not a label.

## The six conditions

1. **Unity and currency of law.** The client applies the canonical law, procedure, bench constitution, and duty of care actually and unaltered, mints no local statute, and renders the central text **live as the apex enacts it** - never a vendored, pinned, or stale local copy whose currency the vendor's release cycle controls. (The moment a client serves its own vendored copy it mints a subscribed local jurisdiction, governed by the federation provisions, whatever its packaging.)
2. **Unity of citator and apex.** Every binding decision resolves to the one central citation series and the single central apex; no parallel series, local apex, or per-domain citator.
3. **The synthesiser bar.** The client constitutes no deciding bench of its own; it only displays rulings handed down by a properly constituted central bench. Displaying a ruling is not constituting one.
4. **Delivery, not law-making.** The client's force is engineering, deriving wholly from the canonical organ by rendering, gating, and explaining - never from promulgation by the client (the source-of-force test).
5. **Candour of provenance.** The re-skinned nomenclature is disclosed to the user, across the whole contemporaneous interface, as descriptive presentation of canonical governance and not as an independent, locally-sovereign body.
6. **The external-law floor / non-impersonation (majority, 7:2).** The client must not wear the appearance of a regulated real-world legal authority it does not hold. This is a floor of substance achieving a result (a reasonable professional viewer is not led to treat the rendering as regulated legal authority), not a script of interface wording; a body of competent practice cannot lower it.

## Disposition

The court would enact the test as ordinary, declaratory law (it creates no new tier, seat, sovereign, citator, or apex), one member dissenting on the need to enact. A proliferation of conforming clients dilutes nothing, because each resolves to the one apex and each must independently satisfy all six conditions on its own facts.
