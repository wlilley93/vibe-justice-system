## Direction 1: mechanical certification is performed by kernel command

Digest verification, operative-text identity between an engrossed and an adopted text,
struct-key conformance of a draft, citation existence, address verification, and count
measurement are CLERK ARITHMETIC. They are performed by kernel commands (`vjs draft check`
before any counsel reads a draft; `vjs certify` for the engrossment certificate), never by
convening a bench.

Benches convene only for JUDGMENT: adoption votes, genuine first-impression forks, appeals,
and discretionary conditions. The measured cause: on 2026-08-05 a sitting of roughly 38k
tokens was convened to verify a sha256 and a diff, and one Act consumed five committee
rounds finding defects a free check now refuses. This direction retires that class.

The commands exist today in a subscribing jurisdiction's vendored kernel and stand offered
upstream under the standing upstream-offer obligation of 2026-08-05 (expires 2026-08-19).
On landing upstream, this direction governs canon drafting practice directly; until then it
governs every drafting act performed where the commands exist.

## Direction 2: bench seats are model-tiered

Where a sitting is convened, its seats are tiered by function: verification and reading
seats run on the economical model tier; the judgment and opinion seats run on the strong
model. One sitting's cost is thereby roughly halved without moving any judgment off the
strong model. The tiering is per-seat configuration of the convening machinery, not a
change to bench sizes or constitution, which remain as constituted.
