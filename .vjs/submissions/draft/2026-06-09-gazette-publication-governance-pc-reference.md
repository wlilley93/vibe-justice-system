## Why now

V2 has commenced (COMMENCEMENT-V2-0001, 2026-06-09). The migration records and the V2 founding sit on the private development line; the public **single Gazette** is hosted in the V1 repository (`vibe-justice-system`). The realm must publish V2 records into that Gazette. But **Bill 32 deliberately left the publication mechanics to a V2 Kernel Regulation that has not been written.** Until it exists there is a gap, and the only on-point mechanic is V1's own release regime (REALM-SI 7 release warrant; REALM-PC 19 publication-route control). The question is which law governs, and the cure.

## The question

> Who governs the publication of V2 records into the single Gazette - V2 (under Bill 32 s.16 and a V2 Kernel Regulation), or V1's preserved release regime (REALM-SI 7 / REALM-PC 19) as the law of the V1-hosted estate - and what governs a push of V2 records to the V1-hosted Gazette repository in the interim, before any V2 publication regulation commences?

Three limbs:

1. **Governance.** Does Bill 32 s.16 (one Gazette, two estates; the force-source rule) together with the Act's delegation of publication mechanics to a Kernel Regulation place publication governance with **V2**, such that V2 must write its own mechanic rather than rely on V1's SI 7?
2. **Transition.** Until that regulation commences, does a push *to the V1-hosted Gazette repo* lawfully proceed under **V1's preserved SI 7 / PC 19** (the V1 estate governing its own repo), or would that be "V1 revival by silence" barred by ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.9?
3. **Cure.** Should the bench direct the adoption, through the V2 lawmaking route, of a **Gazette-Continuity / Publication Regulation** under Bill 32, and what must it contain?

## Proposed doctrine (Lexby's case, to be tested against the contrary case)

- **Governance is V2's.** Bill 32 s.16 is V2 constitutional law over the single Gazette; the force-source rule (publication creates no runtime force) is V2's; the Act expressly delegated the *mechanics* to a V2 Kernel Regulation. So what and whether V2 publishes is V2's to settle, and the durable mechanic must be V2-native.
- **The act of pushing to the V1-hosted repo is governed by the V1 estate's own preserved law (SI 7 / PC 19) in the interim.** That is not s.9 "revival": s.9 bars V1 binding *V2 runtime* by silence; it does not stop V1's law governing V1's own repo. The V1 estate runs its own publication; V2 governs the content and force of what it publishes.
- **The cure is a V2 Gazette-Continuity Regulation** (proposed draft attached) carrying: the single-Gazette / two-estate / force-source rules as operative checks; the publication-packet requirement (manifest, validated records, rendered views, redaction report, validation report, lawpack digest); export-fail-unless conditions; estate labelling operative-by-substance; typed lineage edges; and the rule that a Gazette copy is never the runtime source.

## On all fours, or first-impression?

The **governance and transition limbs are first-impression** under the new settlement: no V2 authority decides cross-estate publication governance, and REALM-SC 10 did not reach it. The **force-source rule itself is settled** (Bill 32 s.16; REALM-PC 24 Q3; REALM-SC 8 source-of-force) and may be applied on citation. The bench should confirm what is settled and decide the rest.

## Requested direction

The V2 Privy Council is asked to: (1) settle that publication governance over the single Gazette is V2's under Bill 32 s.16; (2) hold that, in the interim, a push to the V1-hosted Gazette repo lawfully follows the V1 estate's preserved SI 7 / PC 19 and is not barred by s.9; (3) direct adoption, through the V2 lawmaking route, of a Gazette-Continuity Regulation (the attached draft admitted only as a void first draft); and (4) confirm the force-source rule binds every Gazette entry. Nothing is published by this reference; any public push remains a separately warranted act reserved to the Principal.
