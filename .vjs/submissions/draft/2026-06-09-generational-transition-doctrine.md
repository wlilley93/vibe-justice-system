# DRAFT: The Generational Transition Doctrine (a reusable law of succession between generations)

**status:** VOID FIRST DRAFT, for Committee adoption + Sovereign assent (or, as a binding operational instrument, an SI under the Framework Act s.7). The **declaratory** limbs already bind through their cited entrenched sources; the **operational** limbs need adoption to bind. Drafted by Lexby as Clerk-Drafter, distilling the V1->V2 transition.
**Purpose:** capture the hard-won learnings of the V1->V2 transition as a **generation-agnostic** pattern, so that any future transition V(n) -> V(n+1) follows an established, lawful procedure instead of re-deriving it. The V1->V2 transition is the **worked first instance** and the exemplar; the principles are written for "the prior generation" and "the successor", not for "V1"/"V2".
**Rank limit (manner-and-form only):** this doctrine binds the *method* of a transition as a durable floor against drift; it never absolutely fetters the Sovereign (Bill 10 s.18), and is amendable by specific Sovereign assent citing the section.

---

## Part A - The constitutional relay (declaratory; already entrenched)

1. A successor generation is founded **through the prior generation's own organs** (its drafting committee + Royal Assent), never by self-declaration; the successor cannot bootstrap its own legitimacy. [CASE-LAW s.23; [2026] REALM-PC 24; [2026] REALM-SC 10]
2. The **single apex is preserved**, never fractured; the successor continues the one apex, it does not create a second. [CASE-LAW s.22(2); [2026] VJS-SC 1; [2026] REALM-SC 6]
3. **Adoption is constitutive**: a draft (even one authored by an agent) carries no founding force until the proper organ adopts it; adoption cures the origin defect and is spent on adoption; AI/agents hold no law-making or founding force. [CASE-LAW s.3, s.23(2); [2026] REALM-SC 8]
4. The successor's law requires **Sovereign assent** (specific, digest-bound, or standing-bounded); **commencement is external-then-compiled** - no commencement condition is satisfiable by the new machinery alone, and the kernel never certifies its own warrant. [CASE-LAW s.23; Framework Act s.2, s.10]
5. **No revival by silence**: a gap in the successor's law is a successor first-impression question routed to a court or the lawmaking route, never a silent import of the prior generation's law. [Framework Act s.8, s.20; DEC-V1-SILENCE-001]

## Part B - One register, many estates (operational + declaratory)

6. There is **one Gazette/citator across the generations, with the prior generation as an archival estate**; the graph resolves in one place; a citation to prior-generation law is a **source edge, not an authority edge**. [[2026] VJS-PC 2; Framework Act s.18; REG-GAZETTE-CONTINUITY-001]
7. **Publication is constitutively inert** - force comes from assent and kernel-recognised status, never from being published or carried in the estate. [[2026] REALM-PC 24; Framework Act s.18]
8. The estate boundary is **operative by substance, not caption**: prior-generation records carry machine-readable markers (`estate`, `archival_source_only`, `live_authority: false`) and render distinctly. [[2026] VJS-PC 2; [2026] VJS-PC 3]

## Part C - Restating and incorporating the prior law (operational)

9. **Restate the prior law as a whole** before drafting: a researcher extracts the valid prior law into plain one-sentence principles (each sourced), which the successor adopts as a Schedule; void/superseded/per-incuriam material is excluded, not carried. [Framework Act s.4, Schedule 1; the principles-digest method]
10. **Validity is found entry-by-entry**; nullity is non-infectious and never presumed from age or provenance; a legacy line is conformed prospectively, not bulk-voided. [[2026] REALM-SC 7]
11. Prior-generation law gains live force **only by express incorporation** (an incorporation record + a provenance-scoped migration ledger, one row per crossing), honouring the rank-floor; nothing crosses in bulk or by implication. [Framework Act s.20; REG-MIGRATION-INCORPORATION-001]

## Part D - The migration mechanics (operational; the hardest-won learnings)

12. **Settle the destination first, stage the physical move**: decide topology and the safe sequence separately; defer the byte-move to a governed, lock-preserving instrument. [[2026] VJS-PC 2; REG-CANONICALISATION-MIGRATION-001]
13. **Lock-preserving migration**: assented digests, the lawpack lock, citation series, and record ids are immutable; preserve paths or install a redirect (never a mass edit); **prove each fatal invariant still binds a non-empty set before and after**; record a fresh lock under a commencement addendum (old lock pinned); **halt and return** if continuity or invariant-scoping cannot be proven ("better two repos than a fail-open lawpack"). [[2026] VJS-PC 2; REG-CANONICALISATION-MIGRATION-001; COMMENCEMENT-V2-0002]
14. **Back-fill is transcription, never fabrication**: a missing authority is restored only by word-for-word transcription verified against a digest-pinned source; a record whose native text was never lodged is carried as a clearly-marked **derived minute**, never authored as if original. [[2026] VJS-PC 3]
15. The outward acts (push, rename, tag, re-licence, publish) are **warranted acts reserved to the Principal**, performed under a release warrant with a post-push review; the agent prepares the runbook, never performs the outward act. [[2026] REALM-SI 7; [2026] REALM-PC 16/20]

## Part E - Continuity and the courts (declaratory + operational)

16. **Continuity, not rupture**: perfected orders and accrued rights are preserved; pending matters continue at their current point; nothing is void or relitigated by reason only of the transfer. [[2026] VJS-PC 1; Judicature-Acts model; REG-TRANSITION-CONTINUITY-001]
17. A **transition court** classifies each pending matter (continue / reframe / moot); a narrow **already-heard limb** lets the prior court perfect (publication only) a matter fully heard before commencement, importing nothing. [[2026] VJS-PC 1; Framework Act s.12]

## Part F - The doctrine's own upkeep (new forward duty)

18. **Capture-the-learnings is a standing forward duty**: each generational transition must, on completion, update this Doctrine with what it learned (new failure modes, new safeguards), so the pattern compounds rather than ossifies; the prior generation is preserved and honoured, never found worthless. [CASE-LAW s.8 (forward duty); Framework Act s.23 (lean drafting); Bill 32 s.9]
19. **Manner-and-form only**: this Doctrine constrains the method of transition as a durable floor; it does not fetter the Sovereign's power to make or unmake law, and is amendable by specific Sovereign assent citing the section. [Bill 10 s.18; CASE-LAW s.2]

## Adoption note

The declaratory limbs (Parts A, E, and 19) restate law already entrenched via their cited sources and bind now. The operational limbs (Parts B-D, F) become binding on adoption: either as a **Part of the Framework Act** (if the Committee prefers to fold it in) or as a standalone **Generational Transition Regulation under s.7** (the leaner option, recommended, so it can be amended by SI as future transitions teach more). Either way it is generation-agnostic and is the pattern a future V3 follows. Nothing here binds until adopted + assented; the V1->V2 transition already complied with every limb.
