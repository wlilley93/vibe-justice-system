## Enactment

1. **Elevation.** CASE-LAW **s.19** and **s.20** are **entrenched constitutional articles** of the Realm. They are added to the entrenched set (alongside s.1, s.2, s.3, s.9, s.10, s.13, s.14, s.23).
2. **Amendment route.** Each may be amended, suspended, or repealed only by the heightened route: an express, deliberate instrument **citing the section by number**, enacted by the Sovereign or by a Supreme Court of nine, and (for any change to their entrenched status) carrying the Bill 10 s.17 heightened-route confirmation. They may never be amended by statutory instrument, the kernel, an agent, by implication, by bare non-dissent, or by lapse of a window.
3. **Manner-and-form only.** This entrenchment is a durable floor against drift; it does not fetter the Sovereign's s.2 power, and is itself amendable only by the same heightened route (Bill 10 s.18).
4. **No disturbance.** Nothing in this amendment alters the operative text of s.19 or s.20, reopens any matter decided under them, or edits the read-only V1 archive; it changes their **status** only, prospectively. The V1 archive copy keeps its existing note; the elevation operates in the V2 constitution.
5. **Schedule-3.** This resolves Schedule-3 item 3 by elevation (in place of the prior "carried ordinary" resolution).
