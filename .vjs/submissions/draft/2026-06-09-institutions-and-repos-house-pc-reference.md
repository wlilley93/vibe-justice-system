# V2 Privy Council Reference: institutions, Repos House, and whether to legislate

**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Filed by:** Lexby, on its own motion (self-submission duty; the Principal directed "get a ruling first, maybe this needs legislation too"). NOT decided by Lexby; NOT routed to the Principal to choose.
**Court:** V2 Privy Council (institutional structure / governance / public-private boundary; bench of 3; with leave to escalate if a seat finds the matter foundational).
**Status:** public; system-data only; the questions and rival positions stated neutrally (the intake), no access to Lexby's preference.

## Background (settled law the bench applies)

- The four-branch ministry directory is **not** carried into V2 runtime; functions are configurable record roles (Migration Charter; ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.24; Framework Act Part 8).
- A repo is bound by **local sovereign invocation, not directory ancestry** (REG-INVOCATION-001; ACT-007); the sole anchor is `.vjs/config.toml`.
- A **repos register** already exists in lean form (REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001: mandated centrally, maintained per-repo, derived/pointer-only). The Principal proposes a richer **Repos House** registry/certification service.
- V1's Repos House ([2026] REALM-SI 6, an MBES function) is carried forward as **archival source only**; its learning (private operational registers, public schemas committed, private contents out of the public tree) is to be kept while shedding the ministry packaging.
- Non-disturbance ([2026] VJS-SC 1); the public record is system-data only ([2026] REALM-PC 19 family).

## The questions

**Q1 - Repos House.** Should V2 retain **Repos House** as a lightweight **jurisdiction registry and certification ledger** (records, certifies, discovers, publishes jurisdictions; tracks identity, lawpack lock, invocation status, public/private classification, hook/CI/boundary certification, Gazette status, and federation/fork/independence lineage) that is **declarative, not sovereign** - registration does **not** create legal force (local invocation does), and Repos House does not decide whether a repo is bound, whether a local order is valid on the merits, whether private evidence is public, or whether V1 binds V2? Should it have **two layers** (a private in-repo register holding operational facts; a public Gazette register holding system-data-only metadata)? Should registration be **optional** for private/local repos unless they federate, publish to the Gazette, or claim canonical status? Does it **supersede or extend** REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001?

**Q2 - MoJ and MBES.** Are the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills **dropped as mandatory V2 institutions, paths, or runtime packaging**, their useful functions carried forward as registries and roles - MoJ -> **Court Registry + Gazette Clerk**; MBES -> **Toolchain Registry + Skills/Agent Registry**; the ministry **policy arms** -> a **Policy Proposal record type** routed by the kernel (implementation / regulation / Act / Privy Council / Supreme Court), not a standing office? May V1 names persist as **archive lineage and optional ceremonial aliases with no legal or path effect**?

**Q3 - Vehicle.** Is this settled by **ruling** (as an application of the already-settled directory-agnostic / functions-by-role law), or does it need **legislation** - e.g. a `DEC-INSTITUTIONS-001` (V2 uses registries and roles, not ministries) and a `REG-REPOS-HOUSE-001` (the registry regulation), plus a Skills/Agent registry record - and if legislation, what is its rank and parent authority?

## Rival positions (neutral)

- **Position A (ruling-only, minimal):** the directory-agnostic / functions-by-role law already decides it; a short ruling declaring the registry-and-roles model and reconciling Repos House with REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001 suffices; defer any regulation as non-critical-path (permit enforcement and the lawmaking route come first).
- **Position B (ruling + legislation):** the institutional model is load-bearing enough to crystallise in law - adopt `DEC-INSTITUTIONS-001` + `REG-REPOS-HOUSE-001` (declarative registry; two layers; optional-unless-federating; certification ledger; lineage) + a Skills/Agent registry, all subordinate under the Framework Act s.7, with V1 names archive-only.

## Constraints the bench is asked to honour

Local sovereignty (invocation binds, not the registry); the public/private boundary (public register system-data only); non-disturbance (REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001 and the lodged regulations are not re-cut without cause); leanness (no re-bloat into ministry structure); priority (the Principal's note that Repos House is not the critical path - permit enforcement first). Decide the substance and the vehicle; nothing is built or lodged by this reference.
