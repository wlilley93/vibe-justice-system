## How the matter arrives

In [2026] VJS-PC 1 the V2 Privy Council took jurisdiction over the V1->V2 transition and onboarding, but **leapfrogged the constructive apex/federation question (Q3) as foundational**: a 3-judge bench would not gloss the entrenched single-apex rule (ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.15). The Council recorded one part as **already settled and fast-path**, and reserved the rest to this Court.

## What is already settled (not re-opened)

- **The single apex is entrenched** (s.15; REALM-SC 6 as persuasive archive: the singleness of the apex may never be relaxed; apex enactment is non-delegable).
- **The super-repo survives into V2 as an ACT-007 federation** of peer local jurisdictions under one canonical lawpack and one continued apex (REALM-PC 15, persuasive: the super-repo is the realm-as-state and adds no new tier, seat, or apex).
- **A federation/super-repo root may never (i) stand up a second or parallel apex, nor (ii) bind a peer repo's law beyond ACT-007 s.4 (local-scope-only), nor (iii) override the canonical lawpack without ACT-007 s.3.**

This reference does not ask the Court to disturb any of that.

## The question (the residual constructive limb)

> Within the settled single-apex rule, **how far may a federation or super-repo coordinating root go before coordination hardens into sovereignty** over its peer local jurisdictions? What is the test that distinguishes lawful coordination (administrative/infrastructural) from unlawful sovereignty (legislating for, or subjecting, a peer), given that ACT-007 s.2 makes default subscription to the canonical lawpack the norm and REALM-PC 17 (persuasive) holds default subscription is not perpetual subjection?

Sub-questions for the Court:
1. Is the canonical lawpack governing peers by **default subscription** (s.2) an exercise of *consent-based coordination* (revocable; REALM-PC 17) or a *soft sovereignty* requiring tighter limits?
2. What acts by a root are lawful coordination (e.g. distributing the canonical lawpack, version pinning, shared CI, cross-repo discovery, aggregated Gazette export) and what acts cross into sovereignty (e.g. making peer local law without the peer's adoption; gating a peer's ability to amend local law, fork, or exit)?
3. What is the **bright-line and the standard** the kernel and the courts should apply to test a root's act?

## Proposed doctrine (Lexby's case, to be tested against the contrary case)

A coordinating root acts lawfully where its act is **infrastructural and consent-anchored**: it (a) asserts no apex or final-court function; (b) makes no law binding on a peer's local jurisdiction without that peer's own adoption (ACT-007 s.4); (c) does not override the canonical lawpack without s.3; and (d) leaves every local sovereign free to amend local law, pin or decline a version, fork with declared lineage (s.6), or exit the federation (REALM-PC 17: default subscription is not perpetual subjection). Default subscription is lawful consent-based coordination, not sovereignty, precisely because it is revocable and the local sovereign retains exit. A root crosses into unlawful sovereignty the moment it claims an apex, legislates peer local law without adoption, or forecloses a peer's amendment/fork/exit.

## The second limb (Q4): the holes, and the mechanism to reconcile each

The Principal directs the Court, while seised, to **identify any remaining holes in the V1->V2 settlement and determine the reconciliation mechanism for each** - by V2 ruling, by the V2 lawmaking route, by Principal policy, or **by the V1 Court where (and only where) a hole genuinely requires it**. V1 is not dead; it is the Gazette/Archive estate with its own continuing courts, and some reconciliation may belong there (e.g. an already-heard V1 matter that only the V1 machinery can perfect; a question about V1's own law or estate).

The Court is asked to classify each identified hole into exactly one mechanism:
- **V2 Supreme Court** (foundational, entrenched-article, apex);
- **V2 Privy Council** (routing/constitutional first instance);
- **V2 County Court** (repo-local operational);
- **V2 lawmaking route** (Committee draft + Sovereign assent; for instruments and gaps that need new law, not adjudication);
- **Principal policy** (reversible, low-blast executive choice; non-justiciable);
- **V1 Court** (where the hole touches the V1 estate, an already-heard V1 matter, or V1's own historical law, such that only V1's court can act);
- **no hole / already settled** (dispose on citation).

Candidate holes for the Court to confirm, reject, or supplement (non-exhaustive): the coordination/sovereignty line (Q3 itself); the still-unheard Gazette publication-governance reference; the two directed regulations awaiting Committee adoption + assent; the **"perfect by old machinery" limb** (does perfecting an already-heard V1 matter require the V1 Court, since it is V1's machinery?); **who constitutes/sits as the "V2 transition court"** that classifies pending matters; the **non-convened V2 Court of Appeal** (is there an appellate gap?); the V2 citation series minting (VJS-PC / VJS-REG / VJS-SPEC / VJS-DEC) consistency; and any pending V1 matter live at commencement needing carriage.

## The third limb (Q5): the official way to extract valid general V1 law into the V2 starting substrate

The Principal asks the Court to settle the **official mechanism by which non-repo-specific (general) V1 law that remains valid is extracted from the V1 repos and incorporated into the V2 substrate of starting law.** The skeleton is already enacted and must be applied, not re-decided:

- **Bill 32 s.8** (express incorporation): V1 material gains live V2 force only by a valid V2 record stating `v1_source`, `v2_destination`, `operative_rule`, `kernel_effect`, and `supersession_or_variation`. A general reference or a citation for history is not incorporation.
- **Bill 32 s.9 / INV-NO-V1-GAP-FILLER**: nothing crosses by silence.
- **Migration Charter s.11**: a V1 doctrine is live in V2 only if it appears in the **migration ledger** and has a V2 destination record; the ledger is the single source of truth for what crossed.
- **The void-first-draft rule** (REALM-SC 8): Lexby may draft the incorporation; adoption by the organ is constitutive.

What is **not yet canonical** and is asked of the Court:
1. The **distinction in route**: general/substrate law is incorporated through the **central V2 lawmaking route** (Committee draft + Sovereign assent for primary rank; parent authority for subordinate), entering the canonical lawpack; **repo-specific** local law uses the **continuity election** settled in [2026] VJS-PC 1 (local-scope, recorded in `.vjs/config.toml`). The Court is asked to confirm this split and the test for "general vs repo-specific."
2. The **incorporation instrument form** and a **migration-ledger record** as a deterministic V2 artefact (each crossing: v1_source, v2_destination, operative_rule, kernel_effect, supersession, validity-of-original finding), and an **incorporation-validity check** (a kernel invariant) so an incorporation that omits a required element fails closed.
3. The **"where it is valid" gate**: extraction carries only V1 law that was *itself validly adopted* in V1 (entry-by-entry, REALM-SC 7) and is not spent, overruled, or superseded; void or per-incuriam V1 material is not extractable. Confirming the original's validity may, for contested cases, be the one place a **V1 Court** finding is needed (tying Q5 to Q4).

## The fourth limb (Q6): canonicalisation - "V2" is now canon

V2 has commenced and is the live Realm. The Principal directs that **the "V2" qualifier be removed from the name of the canon**: the live Realm is unqualified (VJS / the Realm / the lawpack / the kernel), and **"V1" survives only as the Archive and Gazette estate label**. The Court is asked to settle, as a constitutional/canon matter:
1. the **principle** - the successor, having commenced, is the unqualified canon; a transitional ordinal ("V2") used to distinguish a pending successor from the incumbent is spent on commencement and is dropped from names going forward (the incumbent, now archive, keeps "V1" only to mark the estate);
2. the **non-disturbance rule** - de-naming is prospective and nominal; it does not reopen, revoke, or alter any enacted record, citation, digest, or assented text (the assented Bill 32 bytes and the `8e1d3f51...` digest are immutable; the `[YEAR] VJS-ACT N` series and existing record IDs are not retro-edited merely to drop "V2"); and
3. the **mechanism** - direct the legislature to enact a short canonicalisation instrument and direct the engineering rename (repo, lawpack path, prose) as a governed change that preserves digests and citations, not a mass edit that breaks them.

## Requested direction

The Court is asked to: (1) settle the coordination/sovereignty test (the bright-line + standard) and confirm or qualify the four-part lawful-coordination formula (Q3); (2) **identify the holes and assign each a single reconciliation mechanism per the list above, expressly flagging any that must go to the V1 Court** (Q4); (3) settle the **official general-law extraction/incorporation pipeline** into the substrate, the general-vs-repo-specific split, and direct a **Migration and Incorporation Regulation** (with a migration ledger and an incorporation-validity invariant) through the lawmaking route (Q5); (4) settle the **canonicalisation** principle, non-disturbance rule, and mechanism, and direct a canonicalisation instrument + the governed rename (Q6); and (5) return the settled rules to the Privy Council and the lawmaking route so the instruments can be completed.

**Codification mandate (the REALM-SC 8 method).** The Court is asked to **rule the principles and DIRECT the Standing Committee to capture all of them in enacted legislation**, so the settlement does not rest as bare Supreme Court precedent. The Court should not legislate the instrument text from the bench (that would repeat the vice REALM-SC 8 condemned); it rules the ratio and directs the legislature, and the binding form is the assented instrument(s). The Principal asks that the Court frame its directions as a clear legislative programme the Committee can draft against. Nothing is migrated, adopted, or published by this reference; the resulting instruments are void first drafts until adopted and assented (CASE-LAW s.23).
