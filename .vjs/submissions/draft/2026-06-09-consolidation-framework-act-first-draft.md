# DRAFT: The Realm Consolidation and Reconciliation Framework Act 2026

**status:** VOID FIRST DRAFT (REALM-SC 8; CASE-LAW s.23(6)). No force until adopted by the Standing Committee and assented by the Sovereign against the final digest-pinned text. Drafted by Lexby as Clerk-Drafter on the principles digest of 2026-06-09, the drafting brief, and the rulings [2026] VJS-PC 1, PC 2, SC 1. Rank: constitutional. Citation: minted at commencement on the VJS-ACT series.
**Drafting standard:** lean and legible; one idea per subsection; the body carries machinery only, the restated law sitting in Schedule 1.

---

## Part 1 - Preliminary

**1. Short title and citation.**
(1) This Act is the Realm Consolidation and Reconciliation Framework Act 2026.
(2) Its V2 ordinal is minted deterministically on the VJS-ACT series at commencement; "[2026] VJS-ACT 8" is a void self-mint and is not used.

**2. Commencement.**
(1) This Act is enacted on Royal Assent and commences on a strict external-then-compiled sequence: Royal Assent under the V1 route, bound by a digest link, is the sole and wholly antecedent legitimating act.
(2) No commencement condition is satisfiable by the V2 machinery alone; lawpack validation, entry, and lock confirm form and digest-fidelity only.
(3) Commencement yields one commencement record pinning the assent event, the assented-text digest, the lawpack version, and the lock.

**3. Interpretation.**
(1) Defined terms are listed in Schedule 4 and govern; a section using an operative term not defined there or in an incorporated record is a validation defect.
(2) A record that bears no kernel effect is not runtime law.
(3) Where a section's ceremonial text and its kernel effect differ materially, the discrepancy is a validation defect routed for correction; the kernel must not silently choose.

## Part 2 - Restatement of the settled law

**4. Adoption of the Restated Principles.**
(1) Schedule 1 (the Restated Principles) restates the settled law of V1 as a whole and is enacted as the substrate of starting law of the Realm.
(2) Each numbered principle in Schedule 1 is a restated provision of this Act.
(3) The V1 source cited against each principle is its archival source, preserved so the citation graph resolves; it is not itself live authority (s.13).
(4) A principle in Schedule 1 marked [CHECK] does not commence until the flagged matter is resolved on the record (Schedule 3).

**5. Effect of restatement.**
(1) Restatement reorganises and states; it does not alter legal meaning.
(2) A material divergence between a Restated Principle and its archival source is a validation defect, not a silent amendment.

## Part 3 - Lawmaking and future adaptation

**6. The lawmaking route.** New law is introduced by proposal or authority basis, draft record, schema validation, authority validation, boundary validation, deterministic citation, authorised adoption, lawpack entry, lock update, and decision log.

**7. Power to make statutory instruments.**
(1) The Standing Committee may make statutory instruments and subordinate regulations under this Act, in exercise of the power conferred by this section.
(2) An instrument made under this section is subordinate law, strictly below this Act and the Acts of Union; it may not amend, disapply, or expand this Act, the constitution, or any primary Act, or amend the assent rule (anti-Henry-VIII).
(3) Primary law requires Sovereign assent; subordinate law requires parent authority traceable to this section or another enabling provision.

**8. Future adaptation.**
(1) Amendment is append-only and express; the old text remains visible; silent repeal and implied importation from V1 are prohibited.
(2) This Act provides standing routes out for future adaptation: statutory instruments (s.7), regulations, practice directions, and registers (s.9).

**9. Registers.**
(1) This Act may mandate that a register exist and fix its schema; the register's contents are **maintained at the lowest competent level** (subsidiarity), not centrally.
(2) A register is a derived, pointer-only, rebuildable projection over the committed record; it is never itself a source of law.

## Part 4 - The assent floor

**10. The constitutive gate.**
(1) Royal Assent is the sole constitutive gate for a primary or constitutional Act; it is non-automatable, non-presumable, and non-delegable.
(2) The computational legislature may propose, draft, codify, consolidate, and revise only; it is not sovereign, may not expand its own competence, sub-delegate, amend the assent rule, or create force from its own output.
(3) A record carries binding runtime force only if it declares an assent_source resolving to a specific digest-bound assent or a traceable standing-bounded route; absence, emptiness, an unrecognised form, or an unresolved trace each cause rejection (the fail-closed allow-list Assent-Source Invariant; no deny-list form is permitted).

## Part 5 - The courts and the transition

**11. Continuity of the courts.** The three-tier hierarchy (First Instance 1; Court of Appeal 3; Supreme Court 5, expandable to 9) and the single apex are carried into V2 in continuity; the Court of Appeal persists and is convened on need; no V2 court issues an order until expressly constituted by a named instrument fixing an odd bench and jurisdiction.

**12. The transition court.**
(1) The Privy Council is the transition court: it classifies each pending V1 matter as continued at its current point, reframed where continuation is unworkable, or moot and closed.
(2) Perfected V1 orders and accrued rights are preserved; no matter is void, restarted, or relitigated by reason only of the transfer.
(3) The V1 Court acts only to perfect a matter already fully heard in V1 before commencement, by perfection in V1 form and Gazette publication only, creating no new doctrine and importing nothing into V2.

## Part 6 - Federation, subscription, and the mandatory transition

**13. Local jurisdictions.** Installing VJS into a repo creates a local jurisdiction anchored at `.vjs/config.toml`; the local Principal may vary local law, pin or decline a version, fork with declared lineage, or exit, binding only locally; local law does not override the canonical lawpack without a Privy Council order or Principal assent.

**14. Coordination and sovereignty.**
(1) A coordinating or super-repo root may federate peers under one canonical lawpack and one continued apex but may not be sovereign over them.
(2) A root act is void if it asserts an apex, binds or gates a peer's local law without that peer's adoption, overrides the canonical lawpack without the s.3 route, or forecloses a peer's amendment, version-decline, fork, or exit (the kernel-checkable bright-line).
(3) Below the bright-line, a root that leverages an infrastructural chokepoint to make a peer's compliance practically unavoidable crosses into sovereignty; that question is for a court, not the kernel.

**15. The mandatory transition.**
(1) This Act binds every repository of the Realm as of commencement; the law applies to them today.
(2) Each repository must perform the internal subscription work - create `.vjs/config.toml`, declare its lawpack lineage, and default-subscribe to the canonical lawpack - within the period fixed by regulation.
(3) Subscription is owed now and is the default; the lawful variation and exit routes (s.13) are preserved.

**16. The repositories register.** There is a repositories register, mandated by this Act and maintained at the federation level but populated by each repository's own subscription act, recording each subscribed repository's lineage and trust status. A project may live in any directory or repository provided it is lawfully subscribed and recorded in the register.

## Part 7 - Canonicalisation and the Gazette

**17. The canon.**
(1) The commenced successor is the unqualified canon (VJS, the Realm, the lawpack, the kernel); the spent "V2" ordinal is dropped from names going forward; "V1" survives only as the Archive/Gazette estate label.
(2) De-naming is prospective and nominal and disturbs no enacted record, citation, digest, record id, or assented text; the assented digest, the lawpack lock, the VJS-ACT and REALM-* series, and existing ids are immutable.
(3) The rename is a governed change preserving digests, citations, and the lock; it is not a mass edit; a path moves only behind a compatibility route or a fresh lock recorded under a commencement addendum, and halts and returns if a path-scoped invariant cannot be proven still to bind.

**18. The single Gazette.**
(1) There is one Gazette with two estates, built on the canonical line and carrying both, so the citation graph resolves in one place.
(2) The V1 Archive estate is anchored by an immutable tag, exposed as a protected read-only branch and a distinct Archive directory; a bare branch is not an estate.
(3) Each V1/REALM record carries machine-readable estate markers (estate: v1_archive; status: archival_source_only; live_authority: false); a citation to a V1 source is a source edge, not an authority edge; publication creates no runtime force.

## Part 8 - Functions by role, not directory

**19. No mandatory ministry directory.**
(1) The four-branch ministry directory is not carried as mandatory runtime structure; a path name creates no legal force.
(2) The functions formerly held by the Ministry of Justice (adjudication, records custody) and the Ministry of Business, Engineering and Skills (engineering, build, repositories) persist as configured roles, discharged by whichever record or agent holds the role, in any directory.
(3) Records bind by role, schema, id, status, authority basis, and kernel effect.

## Part 9 - Incorporation of further V1 law

**20. Express incorporation.** V1 law not in Schedule 1 has live V2 force only by an express incorporation record stating v1_source, v2_destination, operative rule, kernel effect, supersession or variation, and a validity-of-original finding; nothing crosses by silence.

**21. The migration ledger and the validity gate.**
(1) Every incorporation is recorded, one row per crossing, in the migration ledger, which is a deterministic, append-only, provenance-scoped record and the single source of truth for what crossed; the ledger is not loaded into runtime context.
(2) A fail-closed incorporation-validity invariant rejects any incorporation missing a required element, breaching the rank-floor (a lower-rank record may not incorporate higher-rank V1 material), missing a ledger row, or weakening the protective floor.
(3) General (realm-wide) law crosses by the central lawmaking route; repo-specific law uses the local continuity election; doubt resolves to general.
(4) A genuinely contested validity of a V1 source is found by the V1 Court; an uncontested source is certified by the adopting organ on the record.

## Part 10 - Licence

**22. Licence of the canon.**
(1) The Principal, as copyright holder, adopts the GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL) for the canonical codebase of the Realm; this section records that adoption (the licence change being the prerogative of the copyright holder).
(2) An AGPL third-party tool is incorporated only behind a separate-service boundary that does not place AGPL code into a differently-licensed work; a capability of such a tool may instead be recreated clean-room under this Act, driven by a spec, County Court decisions, and the permit/proof/log ledger.

## Part 11 - Duties

**23. Duties carried and owed.**
(1) The agent and office duties restated in Schedule 1 (reasonable skill and care and the graded endeavours; candour and proactive disclosure; obedience only to lawful instruction; capability is not authority; nemo iudex; the duty to offer the lawful route and self-refer) are owed going forward.
(2) It is a forward duty of every drafter and record-author that legislation and records be drafted lean and legible; breach of any duty in this section is a falling-below to be made good, never punished.

## Part 12 - Transitional, savings, severance

**24. Transitional and savings.** Existing REALM-* citations remain valid historical identifiers; V1 material migrates only by incorporation; validly-made prior rulings, citations, statuses, and remedies are saved with their status; a matter commenced before commencement continues under the law then in force unless this Act is more protective.

**25. Severance.** If a provision is held void or unenforceable the remainder continues, save that the assent floor (Part 4) and apex-singleness (s.11) are non-severable and their failure stays commencement.
