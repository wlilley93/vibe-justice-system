## Preliminary, and the threshold the Court must decide first

V2 commenced on 2026-06-09. Two questions were left open by the founding and now press: (a) what happens to **pending V1 matters** and to a project's **existing V1 local precedent** when its jurisdiction transfers to V2; and (b) **how a project is onboarded** to V2 and whether the old super-repo model (REALM-SC 6) reconciles with V2's federation model (ACT-007).

**The Court must first decide its own standing and jurisdiction (kompetenz-kompetenz).** The Principal expressly asks the Council to determine, as a threshold, whether the V2 Privy Council is the competent forum for these questions, or whether any limb is foundational and must be leapfrogged to the V2 Supreme Court (s.13/s.20 analogue, carried into V2 by 2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001), or belongs to a local County Court, or is non-justiciable as a Principal policy call.

## The questions

**Q0 (jurisdiction / standing - threshold).** Does the V2 Privy Council have standing and jurisdiction over: (i) the V1->V2 transitional-continuity doctrine; (ii) the project-onboarding-and-migration mechanism; and (iii) the super-repo / federation reconciliation? For each, is the PC the competent forum, must it leapfrog to the V2 Supreme Court as foundational, or is it a local-County-Court or Principal-policy matter? Who has standing to bring it (the Principal, by reference, through Lexby as officer)?

**Q1 (transitional continuity).** On the model of the Judicature Acts 1873, does the transfer of jurisdiction from V1 to V2 (i) vest the old jurisdiction in the new courts, (ii) preserve perfected V1 orders and accrued rights, (iii) permit already-heard V1 matters to be perfected by the old machinery and published to the Gazette, (iv) continue pending V1 matters in the appropriate V2 court at their current procedural point, (v) allow the V2 court to direct old-style or new-style procedure as a bridge, and (vi) forbid treating any pending matter as void, restarted, or relitigated by reason only of the transfer?

**Q2 (project onboarding).** Is a project onboarded to V2 by ordinary local installation under ACT-007:s1-s2 (the local Principal's authority, not a central court's)? Does a migrating project's existing V1 local precedent carry into V2 only by express incorporation (Bill 32 s.8; barred from automatic revival by s.9), or may the local Principal make a wholesale "continuity election" carrying its local precedent forward at its current state (the 1873 continuation analogue)?

**Q3 (super-repo / federation).** Does the V1 super-repo model (a super-repo over County Court hearing-centres, REALM-SC 6) survive into V2's federation of peer local jurisdictions (ACT-007), and on what condition (it may, provided it does not fracture the single apex carried forward in continuity, ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.15)?

## Proposed doctrine (Lexby's case, to be tested against the contrary case)

- **Q0:** The PC has jurisdiction over the transitional and onboarding *mechanics* (Q1, Q2) as routing/jurisdiction matters; Q3 (the apex/federation reconciliation) is foundational and should be **leapfrogged to the V2 Supreme Court**. Standing lies in the Principal's reference.
- **Q1:** Yes to all six limbs - the 1873 continuity model maps cleanly and is the just transitional rule. Transfer carries pending matters across at their current state; it does not erase them.
- **Q2:** Onboarding is the local Principal's act (ACT-007); V1 local precedent carries by express incorporation by default (s.8/s.9), with an available **continuity election** by the local Principal for wholesale carry-forward, recorded in `.vjs/config.toml` and the local lawpack.
- **Q3:** The super-repo survives as a federation of peer local jurisdictions under one canonical lawpack and one continued apex; it may not stand up a second apex.

## Requested direction

The Council is asked to: (1) determine its own jurisdiction and standing per Q0, leapfrogging any foundational limb; (2) settle the transitional-continuity doctrine (Q1) and direct a Transitional and Continuity Instrument (the attached draft admitted only as a void first draft) through the V2 lawmaking route; (3) settle the onboarding mechanism and the continuity election (Q2); and (4) on Q3, either decide or refer to the V2 Supreme Court. Nothing is migrated or published by this reference; project migration remains the Principal's reversible per-project call.
