## Background (near-on-point law)

- **[2026] REALM-PC 15** (V1, archival source): the super-repo is the **realm-as-state**, not a County Court; it adds no apex; the **central Privy Council is the distinct central first-instance court** (CASE-LAW s.22(3)).
- **REG-INVOCATION-001** (V2): a repo is bound by **local sovereign invocation, not by directory ancestry**; the sole anchor is `.vjs/config.toml`.
- The canon super-repo (`agent-universe-v2`, becoming the canonical line) is the source of the lawpack, yet on the invocation doctrine its *binding* should flow from invocation, not from being the source.
- `vjs invoke` has been run on the canon (lawpack.lock + invocation record lodged), without `--install-hooks`.

## The questions

**Q1 - self-invocation.** Must the canon super-repo be **self-invoked** under REG-INVOCATION-001 so the law binds it like every other jurisdiction (consistency, not exemption), rather than being treated as bound merely because it authors the law? Is the canon's invocation special in any way (it subscribes to its own lawpack)?

**Q2 - first-instance court.** Is the **Privy Council the super-repo's first-instance / County-Court analogue** for realm-level matters (carrying [2026] REALM-PC 15 into V2), so realm-as-state operational matters start at the PC rather than at a County Court the super-repo does not have?

**Q3 - vehicle.** Is this settled by **ruling** (application of REALM-PC 15 + REG-INVOCATION-001) and a short **order** designating the PC as the realm-as-state's first-instance tier and recording the canon's self-invocation, or does it need fresh legislation?

## Rival positions (neutral)

- **A (inherent binding):** the canon is the source of the law and is bound inherently; self-invocation is circular and unnecessary; the PC's role is already settled by REALM-PC 15, needing no V2 order.
- **B (invocation binds; order records):** binding flows from invocation, not authorship; the canon self-invokes like any jurisdiction (no exemption, full consistency, and it removes any "the source is above the law" gap); the PC is its first-instance analogue per REALM-PC 15 carried into V2; a short order records both, so the realm binds itself before it goes public.

## Constraints

Single apex never relaxed (the PC is first-instance, not a new apex; CASE-LAW s.22(2)); no exemption of the canon from its own law (the rule-of-law / nemo-est-supra-leges principle); non-disturbance; leanness (an order, not a ministry). Decide substance and vehicle; nothing is invoked or ordered by this reference.
