## The question answered

The 127 enter the register through the ordinary `SignOff` row with NO schema change to carry a locus, because on all 127 the locus the Principal chose IS the frame's own node (selectedLocus.depth = 0 and locus_id = node_id on every one), so `node_id` already names it; what has to change is the front door, which today implements only limb (a) of CC-OPBOX 6's disjunctive test and therefore refuses 118 of the 127 outright and admits the other 9 on the strength of the machine-implanted layer the Principal expressly overrode. Limb (b) is opened with a PER-FRAME evidence binding to each decision's own digest - using the `SignOffEvidence` machinery that already exists and already carries all 19 live rows, narrowed from its present ledger-wide aggregate scope, which is exactly what CC-OPBOX 7 R5 held is not a label-resolution act.

## Ratio

Where a register refuses a Principal act for want of a field, the court measures the act before it amends the schema. A locus that is the subject's own node is already named by the row's subject identifier, and a register that records the subject records that locus; the remedy for an unregistrable act is then not a new field nor a new row kind but the missing limb of the test the register already had to apply. Where binding law makes registration conditional on A OR B and the instrument implements A alone, the instrument does not merely under-enforce, it INVERTS the law, because limb A is satisfied by precisely the artefacts limb B exists to displace: a door that admits only labelled subjects admits only the labels, and where the labels were machine-implanted the door admits the implant and refuses every subject the Principal cleaned. The cure is to read the disjunction in, and the evidence a limb-B row carries must be per-subject and never an aggregate over the population, an aggregate attesting to a record that CLASSIFIES its subjects being no act upon any one of them. And a later Principal refusal of a subject at the very digest a live row binds REVOKES that row: a pleading that refusals "create no register row on any construction" is true of the row they do not create and false of the row they destroy, so a register that still reports those subjects CURRENT is asserting authority the Principal has withdrawn.
