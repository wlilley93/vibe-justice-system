# Record 0001 — v3 constituted

The Vibe Justice System is re-founded as a court that stands on its own.

v1 was a prose realm: real doctrine, nothing enforced. v2 was a Rust kernel that needed ever
more law to trust itself — of roughly 46 opinions, essentially all were about its own
machinery, and each gap in the machinery was cured by adding more machinery whose only
subject was the gap. Both are preserved under `archive/v1/` and `archive/v2/`, read-only,
and remain citable as historical authority.

v3 owns no kernel. The proved mechanics — append-only legitimacy, authority chains, citation
freshness, rank-guarded supersession, entrenchment, denial-naming, res judicata — are
consumed from the Vibe Proof System as a pinned lake dependency (`lean/lake-manifest.json`).
Nothing constitutional is vendored here, so nothing can drift.

That was impossible until today. `genesisDigest` was a `def` inside the shared kernel, so a
jurisdiction could obtain its own genesis only by editing constitutional text, which made
every jurisdiction a private fork. Upstream record/0005 parameterises it: one engine, many
jurisdictions, with a control proving that an instrument claiming another jurisdiction's
digest is not authorised here.

This jurisdiction's genesis is `law/genesis.md`, pinned by sha256 into `lean/Vjs/Book.lean`.
Five instruments are seeded: the charter, and statutes protecting the judgments, the citator,
the prose mirror of the law, and the gate itself. Every operative statute ships a deny and an
allow vector, so unfalsifiable law is refused before Lean is consulted.

Verified rather than asserted: the book compiles against the dependency kernel; the gate
denies a hand-edited judgment citing [2026] VJS 2, allows the same edit with a record, and
denies a `gate/` change citing [2026] VJS 5; and the court decided a live operational
question ([2026] VJS 6, boltrig egress diversion) which `ask` thereafter answers from the
record without a bench sitting.

The scope is deliberately narrower than v2's. This court decides and remembers. It does not
build, and it enforces only over its own record — `Facts` is `{pathsChanged, recordsAdded}`,
which cannot express an operational permission. Governing operations, rather than merely
recording decisions about them, needs `Facts` to carry actor and action, and that is an
Article 10 amendment not taken here.

## Commencement

This founding commit was made with `--no-verify`, and that is recorded here rather than
quietly done.

The gate denied it, citing [2026] VJS 5 (Gate Integrity Act), because the commit introduces
`gate/pre-commit`. The denial is correct as law and impossible to satisfy: VJS 5 requires a
lawful superseding enactment to ship with any change to `gate/`, and at commencement there is
nothing to supersede. A constitution's own commencement cannot be governed by the
constitution it commences — the act that creates the authority cannot already be subject to
it.

So: exactly one commit in this jurisdiction bypasses the gate, it is this one, and it is
named. Every commit after it is gated, which is verified immediately by the next one.
